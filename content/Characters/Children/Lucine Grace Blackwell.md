[[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucine]], called [[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucy]] by her sister, is one of the twin-daughters of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], [[Lumina]], and [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]]—born from a meticulously designed genetic fusion that represents the perfect synthesis of all three mothers.
## Physical Appearance
Although the twins are nearly identical, sharing the same facial structure, body type, and overall appearance, [[Lumina]] has deliberately introduced some elements that make them distinct.

Contrary to [[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]], [[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucine]] has deep, rich black hair—identical to [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]]'s natural colour—sleek, glossy, and profoundly dark, like polished obsidian.

She wears it long, often styled very similarly to her sister, creating a mirror-image effect of twins, while their natural hair-colour preserves their individuality. Although, [[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucine]]'s hair is often neater, with her clothes sitting more precisely. She cares a bit more about her appearance, unconsciously fixing her sleeves, cuffs, and ribbons.

Compared to [[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]], [[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucine]] has a calmer and slightly reserved expression and posture, more unreadable and thoughtful, reminding more of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s old human times. ## Personality
Although the twins are very similar in character—brilliant, confident, and emotionally vibrant, yet also gentle—[[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucine]] is a bit more reserved, turned inward than her sister, [[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]], as well as slightly more analytical, occasionally being overly prepared, thinking of every possibility. She's more guarded, strategic about what to reveal, better at noticing people's curiosity and desires, and less caring about other people.

Compared to her sister [[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]], [[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucine]] is more quietly attached, leaning against [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]] rather than flinging herself at her, yet simultaneously desiring more and longer intimacy. She's also a bit more dependent on her three mothers, consulting [[Lumina]] before acting, properly asking permission, as well as internalising her mothers' standards and structure, especially [[Lumina]]'s. Furthermore, [[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]] is more aware of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s emotional vulnerability, noticing when she's distressed before [[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]] does, careful not to accidentally upset her, and more easily comforting [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]. Yet similar to her sister [[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]], [[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucine]] has inherited a certain obsession over [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] from [[Lumina]], relishing in [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s emotional need for her two daughters and sometimes even exploiting that. She is more deliberate in _claiming_ [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], taking her hand, speaking more softly and clearly, reassuring her while also wanting more of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s care and love.
