[[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucine]] is one of the twin-daughters of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], [[Lumina]], and [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]]—born from a meticulously designed genetic fusion that represents the perfect synthesis of all three mothers.
## Physical Appearance
Although the twins are nearly identical, sharing the same facial structure, body type, and overall appearance, [[Lumina]] has deliberately introduced some elements that make them distinct.

Contrary to [[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]], [[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucine]] has deep, rich black hair—identical to [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]]'s natural colour—sleek, glossy, and profoundly dark, like polished obsidian.

She wears it long, often styled very similarly to her sister, creating a mirror-image effect of twins, while their natural hair-colour preserves their individuality.
## Personality
Although the twins are very similar in character—brilliant, confident, and emotionally vibrant, yet also gentle—[[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucine]] is a bit more reserved, turned inward than her sister, [[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]], as well as slightly more analytical, occasionally being overly prepared, thinking of every possibility.
