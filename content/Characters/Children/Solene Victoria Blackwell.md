[[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]] is one of the twin-daughters of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], [[Lumina]], and [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]]—born from a meticulously designed genetic fusion that represents the perfect synthesis of all three mothers.
## Physical Appearance
[[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]] has long, flowing platinum-white hair—an exact match to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s natural hair before her transformation. It cascades like liquid silver, shimmering faintly in the light.

They both wear their hair long, often styled very similarly, creating a mirror-image effect of twins, while their natural hair-colour preserves their individuality.
## Personality
Although the twins are very similar in character—brilliant, confident, and emotionally vibrant, yet also gentle—[[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]] has inherited a bit more of [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]]'s fierce passion, being more active, impulsive, and pushing forward compared to her sister, [[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucine]].
