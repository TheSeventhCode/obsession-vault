[[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]], called [[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Sol]] by her sister, is one of the twin-daughters of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], [[Lumina]], and [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]]—born from a meticulously designed genetic fusion that represents the perfect synthesis of all three mothers.
## Physical Appearance
[[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]] has long, flowing white hair—an exact match to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s natural hair before her transformation. It cascades like liquid silver, shimmering faintly in the light.

They both wear their hair long, often styled very similarly, creating a mirror-image effect of twins, while their natural hair-colour preserves their individuality. Yet [[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]] might sometimes be a bit messier, with loose strands or her clothes slightly less pristine by the end of the day.

Compared to her sister [[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucine]], [[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]]'s expression and posture is far more open and direct, she's more immediately engage. She walks faster, makes bigger gestures, more immediate eye contact, moving first and trusting her instincts. She's more expressive, with a warmer and more impulsive rhythm, laughing faster and blurting things out when excited, yet behind her outward openness, she's just as calculated as her sister.
## Personality
Although the twins are very similar in character—brilliant, confident, and emotionally vibrant, yet also gentle—[[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]] has inherited a bit more of [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]]'s fierce passion, being more active, impulsive, and pushing forward compared to her sister, [[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucine]]. She's socially bold, deflecting with her charm and confidence, occasionally getting impatient with social stupidity and norms that make no sense to her.

[[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]] is more physically active than [[Lucine Grace Blackwell|Lucine]], casually throwing herself into [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]]'s or [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s arms, she's a bit louder and more alive. She wants to impress [[Lumina]], solving things quickly for her approval, occasionally pushing forward too fast and needing to be reined in. [[Lumina]]'s praise lands on her like sunlight, her disappointment cutting deeply. [[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]] is the twin most likely to test her limits, sometimes deliberately inviting [[Lumina]] or [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]]'s correction. When excited, she drags [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] into things, is less intimidated by [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s scale and form, more likely to clamber all over her when younger, and shamelessly exploiting [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s softness and helplessness to her daughters — she has inherited [[Lumina]]'s more possessive nature, especially over [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]. [[Solene Victoria Blackwell|Solene]] _claims_ [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] more actively and physical, hugs and clings to her, making [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] hold her, using cheers, insistence, and affection.
