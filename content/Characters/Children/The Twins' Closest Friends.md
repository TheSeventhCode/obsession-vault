## Group & Family Dynamics

From their early years at St. Aurelia’s Academy and continuing into adulthood, Solene and Lucine naturally attract a devoted inner circle of peers. While the twins are socially popular and maintain many acquaintances, there is a set of especially close friends that form their core group. In any other generation at St. Aurelia's, each of these students would've likely been crowned the undisputed prodigy of their respective discipline, be it in the sciences, arts, or athletics. Instead, they find themselves perpetually outshone by the effortless, overwhelming brilliance of the Blackwell twins. All of them are part of the global elite—accustomed to immense wealth, private security, dynastic expectations, and no door being closed to them—yet compared to Solene and Lucine, they—just like any other normal human—are fundamentally ordinary.

Rather than breeding bitter resentment, as some few other students at the academy have done, this dynamic forges a loyal bond — the closeness also extending to their families. The parents of these students are among the first at St. Aurelia's to discard the elitist prejudice against the “new money” stigma—some of them being new money themselves—or the unconventional two-mother household, eventually becoming close friends and allies of Elizabeth, and to a rarer extent, Alexandra. Whether initially drawn by curiosity, shared interests in science or fashion, or the sheer gravitational pull of the twins' personalities, their friendships quickly evolve into a genuine, strong loyalty in both directions. Whether bonded through Elizabeth's fashion empire, Alexandra's scientific legacy, or shared board seats on the Academy's foundation, these families act as a social buffer for the Blackwells in high society.

These close friends have accepted that Solene and Lucine are superior in almost every discipline, having found it far more worth to have fun with the two brilliant girls rather than trying to seriously compete with them or taking it the wrong way being unable to beat them. Their parents are glad of the close friendship, since Solene and Lucine regularly tutor their friends in subjects they might struggle in.

To the friends, Elizabeth is the stylish, approachable, and fiercely competent mother, while Alexandra (in her Skinsuit disguise) is viewed as a brilliant, excentric, intimidatingly poised, and _heavily_ modified enigma.

### Childhood to Adolescence

In their early years at the Academy, the group functions as an unstoppable pack of energetic, hyper-competent children. As they transition into their teenage years, however, the internal dynamics grow more layered. While the fierce, playful rivalries at the Academy, concert halls, and sports arenas remain the backbone of their daily interactions, some romantic tensions begin to surface.

Two of the boys—Julian Vance and Caspian Sterling—along with the quietest girl in the group, Clara Vonnegut, gradually develop genuine, deep-seated crushes on either Solene or Lucine. What starts as simple admiration for the twins' intellect, confidence, and effortless competence, slowly transforms into teenage infatuation, leading to moments of awkward tension, fierce protectiveness, and quiet yearning—all of which Lumina (as well as Alexandra and Elizabeth) monitors with a mixture of maternal amusement and vigilance through the twins' brain links.

### The Twins' Mothers

The group's relationship with the twins' parents is a defining aspect of their bond. Being among the few people from the outside world that have far more contact with both Elizabeth _and_ Alexandra, as well as being far closer to the Blackwell family than almost anyone else, they are simultaneously more used to various odd aspects of the Blackwell family, but also notice some of the more hidden oddities.

All of them adore Elizabeth, finding her warm, stylish, understanding she's the definition of attraction, and refreshingly grounded compared to their own often rigid, aristocratic parents. The girls in the group who love fashion view Elizabeth as an absolute icon, thrilled to have such casual access to a world-renowned designer who otherwise seems almost impossible to get access to. With Elizabeth being a completely synthetic lifeform by Lumina's nanobots and basically frozen in her young artificial body, the students understand early on that Elizabeth is a _very_ attractive woman.

Alexandra, however, represents an entirely different phenomenon. To the academically and scientifically inclined members (or those whose parents are in these areas), the elusive Dr. Alexandra Blackwell—the reclusive genius behind compact fusion—is a living legend. Whenever she makes a rare appearance in her Skinsuit disguise, or when the group is finally permitted to visit the Blackwell mansion after intense vetting by Lumina, the scientifically minded children are initially struck with intellectual awe. While they clearly notice the rather unusual reactions Alexandra causes every time another adult sees her, they just notice that she does look rather special with her extremely curvy, tall body.

However, as the group hits puberty and enters their teenage years, a drastic shift occurs in how they perceive the twins' second mother. As children, they simply registered Alexandra as “tall, extremely pretty, and very different”. As hormonal teenagers—especially the three boys—they suddenly become acutely, painfully aware of just how impossibly obscene and hyper-sexualised Alexandra's disguised body is, clearly understanding the reaction of their parents in the past.

Confronted with a woman over 180 centimetres tall, possessing beach-ball-sized breasts, an impossible 30-centimetre waist, and a massive, jutting arse—all paired with an unearthly, doll-like facial beauty and a simulated voice dripping with sultry, velvety resonance—their teenage brains simply short-circuit. Whenever Alexandra enters a room or speaks a single sentence through her Skinsuit's speakers, the boys (and even some of the girls) struggle to form coherent sentences, their faces flushing scarlet as they desperately try to not stare at her absurd curves. It is during these awkward years that the friends finally understand why their own powerful, composed fathers and mothers always seemed flustered, destabilised, and distracted whenever Dr. Blackwell attended an Academy gala. Yet, the more the twins' friends spend time with their mothers, the more they see where the brilliance of Solene and Lucine comes from, some even coming to recognise certain behaviour they appreciate, like, or even love at the twins, within the behaviour of Alexandra or Elizabeth.

To Solene and Lucine, this shift doesn't go unnoticed. While protective of their _Mama's_ anxiety, the twins also find their friends' sudden inability to function around Alexandra endlessly hilarious, occasionally teasing them mercilessly about it while Alexandra remains internally mortified and clinging to Lumina and her wife for composure — who most often are just as amused as her daughters.

### Parents

Over the years, the parents of these children—ranging from tech billionaires to old-world dukes—become the Blackwells' strongest allies in high society. Recognising the immense value of the Blackwell foundation and the terrifying competence of Elizabeth and Alexandra (which sometimes is the terrifying power of Lumina), these families form a protective social buffer around them. In turn, Alexandra, Elizabeth, and even Lumina slowly lower their guard, coming to genuinely trust several of these parents as true family friends.

### 1. Julian Vance (Tech Heir)

- **Age**: Same age as the twins.
- **Background**: Son of Marcus Vance, one of the most powerful silicon-valley tech billionaires and aerospace pioneers in the world. “New Money” like the Blackwells, though far more public.
- **Main Discipline**: Robotics, Computer Science, and Applied Engineering.
- **Dynamic**: Primarily rivals Lucine in engineering and coding, though frequently clashes with Solene in applied robotics. In any other school, Julian would be considered a once-in-a-generation technical genius: at St. Aurelia's, he is perpetually second to the twins. He idolises Alexandra as the creator of the compact fusion reactor—somewhat thrown off-course when he learns who Dr. Alexandra Blackwell “really” is—and was the first to befriend the twins purely out of desperation to know if the rumours about their mother was true.
- **Teenage Developments**: Julian develops a massive, poorly concealed crush on Lucine. He loves her quiet, strategic mind and is the only person outside the family who can occasionally predict her far-ahead thinking. Around Alexandra, Julian is a disaster; his lifelong scientific hero-worship collides violently with his teenage hormones, leaving him in disbelief how a woman looking like Alexandra does, be the brilliant physicist — stammering and staring intensely at the floor whenever she speaks to him.

### 2. Lady Seraphina “Sera” Ashcroft (Aristocrat)

- **Age**: Six months older than the twins.
- **Background**: Daughter of the Duke of and Duchess of Westmarch, representing one of the oldest, most entrenched aristocratic bloodlines in Europe. She possesses impeccable manners, reads social hierarchies effortlessly, and knows how to steer conversations in any direction she desires.
- **Main Discipline**: Equestrian Show Jumping, Fencing, European languages, and Classical Debate.
- **Dynamic**: Solene's ultimate rival in physical sports, riding, and leadership. Sera was raised to rule every room she entered, and when the seven-year-old Blackwell twins first arrived and effortlessly unseated her at the top of the riding and fencing rankings, she challenged Solene on the spot. Instead of becoming enemies, Solene's fiery boldness and passion won Sera over quickly. Sera's complete disregard for her peers' anti-Blackwell snobbery effectively broke the old-money boycott against the twins and family in their first year.
- **Interests & Connections**: Sera is deeply obsessed with haute couture and fashion design, practically worshipping Elizabeth, treating visits to her boutique or the Blackwell mansion like pilgrimages. Her parents, the Duke and Duchess, become Elizabeth's closest allies at the Academy, delighted by Elizabeth's wit and utterly fascinated by Alexandra's rare appearances and her absurd uniqueness.

### 3. Caspian Sterling (Old-Money Golden Boy)

- **Age:** One year older than the twins.
- **Background**: Heir to the Sterling-Meyer banking dynasty, a family whose financial reach spans three centuries and half the globe.
- **Main Discipline**: Athletics (Swimming, Rowing, Track), Macroeconomics, Geopolitics, and Mathematics.
- **Dynamic**: Rivals Solene on the athletic pitches and Lucine in academic economics and strategy. Caspian was raised to be the stoic, untouchable golden boy of his cohort. But the twins have completely dismantled his cool exterior by beating him in the pool and out-arguing him in seminar rooms before they even turned ten. Being around them allowed Caspian to drop his suffocating dynastic facade, actually relax a bit, and act like a kid.
- **Teenage Developments**: Caspian develops a strong, genuine feelings for Solene, drawn to her explosive energy, warmth, and fearless nature—the exact opposite of his rigid upbringing. Her freedom to simply _do_ whatever she wanted, and possessing the will and ability to actually do so fascinates him, wanting to join and share this freedom with her. This crush doesn't go unnoticed by his parents, who after some deliberation actually support his romantic interest, believing Solene would make an excellent partner for the future head of their banking dynasty, and become mutual supports for each other's ambitions. He is fiercely protective of both twins, even if neither really needs it. In his teens, Caspian is perhaps the most visibly affected by Alexandra's disguised appearances; accustomed to the understated, conservative women in the finance world, Alexandra's pornographic hourglass proportions and hypnotic voice leave him completely tongue-tied and blushing furiously, yet also recognising the same will and freedom of Solene in how Alexandra doesn't care what others think about her obscene appearance.

### 4. Clara Vannegut (The Quiet Virtuoso)

- **Age**: Same age as the twins.
- **Background**: Daughter of a world-renowned Austrian symphony conductor and billionaire pharmaceutical heiress.
- **Main Discipline**: Classical Music (Piano and Violin) and Physics.
- **Dynamic**: Rivals both twins in the music conservatory, particularly Lucine with whom she frequently plays duets. Clara is naturally shy, anxious, and easily overwhelmed by the cutthroat social posturing of St. Aurelia's. The twins took her under their wing early on, shielding her from bullies without a second thought. Because Clara is so gentle and quiet, she is the friend Alexandra is most comfortable around, as Clara's soft presence reminds Alexandra of her own social anxieties.
- **Teenage Developments**: As they enter their teens, Clara's deep gratitude and admiration for Solene blossoms into a quiet, devoted romantic crush. Solene's bright, protective warmth acts as a sun that Clara could endlessly orbit around. Even though she is shy, Clara is the one who most often gets invited to practise music or simply hang out at the Blackwell mansion, where Alexandra (via her Skinsuit) occasionally teaches her and the twins music—a situation that makes Clara adore the Blackwell family even more.

### 5. Matteo Rossini (Charming Strategist)

- **Age**: A few months younger than the twins.
- **Background**: Son of an Italian industrialist billionaire (luxury automotive and shipping empires) and a former international supermodel.
- **Main Discipline**: Astrophysics, Chess, and Sailing.
- **Dynamic**: Lucine's primary rival over the chessboard and in the astrophysics dome. Matteo is effortlessly charming, a natural flirt, and a brilliant thinker who treats life somewhat too often like a game—until he plays Lucine, who systematically crushes his strategies every single time. He finds losing to the Blackwell twins hilarious and exhilarating, finally having found _real_ challenge in his games, acting as the group's comic relief and social lubricant.
- **Interests & Connections**: Growing up around supermodels and hyper-cars, Matteo is immediately obsessed with the Blackwell's custom vehicle, the _Lumen_, and Alexandra's general engineering projects. In his teenage years, Matteo is the most vocal (when at least believing to be out of earshot of the twins) about how ridiculously hot the twins' mothers are, both Elizabeth and especially Alexandra. Though the moment Alexandra actually looks at him with the poised, imposing gaze of her Skinsuit disguise, his Italian bravado evaporates almost entirely, realising that even compared to the many stunning women he had already met in his life by his parents' connections, the two Blackwell women and especially Alexandra were in a league of their own.

### 6. Amara Al-Jamil (Diplomatic Powerhouse)

- **Age**: Same age as the twins.
- **Background**: Daughter of a Middle Eastern royal-adjacent energy magnate and a high-ranking United Nations diplomat.
- **Main Discipline**: Linguistics, International Law, Tennis, and Martial Arts.
- **Dynamic**: Rivals both twins in language acquisition (all three of them speaking upwards of six languages fluently by their early teens) and spars with Solene in the martial arts studio. Amara is sharp, perceptive, and immune to bullshit. She befriended the twins because they were the only students at St. Aurelia's who didn't treat her family's oil-and-energy background as a political transaction and seemed like actually _genuinely_ nice people to be around without any sort of agenda.
- **Interests & Connections**: Because her father's empire transitioned from fossil fuels to licensing Alexandra's compact fusion technology, Amara's parents hold Dr. Blackwell in very high esteem. She is fascinated by the dynamic between Elizabeth and Alexandra, admiring how Elizabeth commands social spaces while Alexandra seemingly holds the quiet, world-changing power in the background. Her parents become some of the must trusted confidants of Elizabeth and Alexandra outside the mansion.

### 7. Chloé Laurent (Avant-Garde Creative)

- **Age**: Same age as the twins.
- **Background**: Daughter of the owners of the largest luxury fashion and cosmetics conglomerate in Paris.
- **Main Discipline**: Fine Arts, Textile Design, Photography, and Ballet.
- **Dynamic**: Rivals Solene and Lucine in the ballet studio and art ateliers. Chloé is eccentric, observant, and possesses an incredible eye for aesthetics and human movement. While she can never quite match the twins' supernatural physical grace in ballet (inherited from their mothers' rigorous training, Lumina's bioengineering, and sophisticated training from early on), her artistic vision is unmatched among the regular students.
- **Interests & Connections**: As the hair to a fashion empire and a passionate designer herself, Chloé is ecstatic to be friends with Elizabeth Carter's daughters, constantly seeking Elizabeth's critique on her sketches. However, as an artist and dancer, Chloé is also deeply, quietly fascinated by Alexandra. She is the first in the group to notice that despite Alexandra's seemingly artificial, extreme plastic surgery, Dr. Blackwell moves with the absolute, impossible biomechanical perfection of a master prima ballerina at all times, bewildered how someone could keep up that composure indefinitely. While the boys stare at Alexandra's out of teenage lust, Chloé stares at her artistic bewilderment, constantly trying—and failing— to sketch how a body shaped like that can balance, glide, and move with such weightless poise.
