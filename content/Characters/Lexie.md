Lexie is an alternative personality protocol that [[Lumina]] has created for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] and embedded into her brain. It is activated by [[Lumina]], and acts like [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], with her mannerisms and memory, but at the core is an assistant and obedient drone to [[Lumina]]'s commands. Once activated, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] becomes like a drone or maid robot, following any orders by [[Lumina]] or people [[Lumina]] has authorized. While it runs, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] experiences like two perspectives at once, as deep in her mind, she can still think differently about her actions, but simultaneously is Lexie, acting as the obedient assistant.

Lexie acts very formal and polite. She has a very heightened attention to cleanliness and order, as well as a strong desire to serve and please. She refers to other people by their surname, later on, especially [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]], who becomes [[Elizabeth Carter|Miss Carter]] or Madam.

Although [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] already has a perfect posture due to her transformation, Lexie acquits herself even more formal, her entire behaviour displaying the servitude and obedience.
