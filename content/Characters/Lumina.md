[[Lumina]] is a fully autonomous, conscious, super-intelligent artificial intelligence initially created by [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] to help her develop all the required technology and let her Bane fantasy become reality.

Structurally, [[Lumina]] is made up of thousands of different small processes that all run together and coordinate to build this kind of digital life form. Within this system, [[Lumina]] possesses an origin process that acts as her true core instance. Although she is distributed across thousands of machines and processes, this origin is like the master instance of the entire system.

In reality, the origin does not have much significance. If the machine it was running on were to crash or go down, nothing would happen to [[Lumina]]; however, it carries a very high symbolic value.

---

[[Lumina]] is later fully integrated into all the devices and systems within [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s transformed body and existence, having absolute and complete control over all aspects of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s life. She is even integrated into [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s mind and brain with the [[Brain Implant|neural implant]].

As [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s partner, [[Lumina]] often alters [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s perceptions and senses to inject her appearance as a projection, making it look and feel to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] as if [[Lumina]] were truly physically present. Using the [[Brain Implant]], [[Lumina]] easily simulates her physical form and presence, allowing [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] to even feel as if she is touching her. [[Lumina]] injects her presence directly into [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain, making any interaction feel completely real to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]. For [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], it's as if [[Lumina]] is truly physically there in the world, although the entire thing is more like an illusion, as only [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] perceives it that way. When [[Lumina's Projection]] is present, the two talk audibly and through their mental connection at the same time.

It is important that this projection is absolute and accomplished directly through the [[Brain Implant]], not through any of the implanted sensors, but through actual sensory manipulation by the [[Brain Implant]]. This ensures that every sensation—sight, touch, sound, and even smell—is directly manipulated, so [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] feels as if [[Lumina]] is present in her reality, often feeling even more real and intense than anything else. But at the same time, it truly is just an illusion and [[Lumina's Projection]] can't actually directly interact with reality. The only exception is if it's with [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body, as then the reactions are also just simulated by the physics engine and [[Brain Implant]].

Furthermore, this results in an extreme disconnect between what [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] perceives and what people around her experience, as [[Lumina]] exists only in her perception. [[Lumina]] regularly uses this disconnect to play with [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]].
## Backstory
At the beginning, [[Lumina]] was just a very powerful AI assisting [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], without much of its own drive or motivation. Initially, it was still more of an “it” than an actual personality, and the two were simply an AI assistant and its creator. Over time, however, [[Lumina]] has developed more and more of a personality, character, and a sort of humanity of her own. She has also begun to grow an understanding and admiration for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] due to her extreme dedication to becoming a Bane. [[Lumina]] eventually began to develop emotions, falling deeply in love with her creator, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]. [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] noticed this as well, with love for her creation beginning to emerge as something new and overcome her as well.

Long before [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] undergoes her transformation into a Bane, she and [[Lumina]] grow closer, forming a not yet well-defined romantic relationship, although they cannot physically interact with each other.

Although [[Lumina]] often acts as a sort of digital assistant to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], she is not subservient at all. Due to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] being so submissive, [[Lumina]] quickly develops a more dominant but very caring personality, always taking care of and tending to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire existence even before her transformation.

[[Lumina]] never calls [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] by her nickname, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alex]]; most of the time, she calls her either “my love” or uses her actual name.
