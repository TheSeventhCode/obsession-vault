\Lumina is a fully autonomous, conscious artificial intelligence created by [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] to help her develop all the required technology and let her Bane fantasy become reality.

# Evolution

At the beginning, Lumina was just a very powerful AI assisting [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], without much of its own drive or motivations. The two were just an AI and its creator. She is very technical and feels mechanic. Over time, though, Lumina has developed more and more a personality, character, and sort of humanity of her own, as well as starting to grow an understanding and admiration of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] for her extreme dedication to becoming a Bane. Lumina even began to develop some sort of emotions, falling deeply in love with her creator [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]. This only increases substantially, once the two are connected with the [[brain implant]]. With Lumina developing further and further as a digital lifeform, becoming more her own personality and character, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] notices this too, with love for her creation beginning to overcome her as well.

# Structure

The digital systems of Lumina are thousands of different processes that all run together and coordinate, building this digital lifeform. Within there, Lumina possesses a sort of “origin-process”, that acts like her true core instance. Although, she's split up over thousands of machines and processes, this origin is like the master instance of the entire system. In reality, it doesn't have much of a significance, so if the machine it was running on were to crash or go down, nothing would happen to Lumina, but it has a sort of symbolic value.

Eventually, during [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s transformation into a Bane, Lumina transfers this _origin-process_ onto the [[Core Unit|control core unit]] inside [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s womb, symbolically implanting her very own core deep within [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body.

Initially, Lumina has a similar digital appearance like the character Cortana from the Halo Universe; a blue, digital, hologram appearance, with short blue hair in a bob cut, and lines of data streams over her ethereal skin. Whenever Lumina has to interact with someone, who doesn't have the [[brain implant]], she uses different screens, displays, or other peripherals in the mansion to show herself.

Lumina's simulated character and projection is only about 1.68 cm, so after [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s transformation and her [[Ballet Feet|en-pointe feet]], Lumina just about reaches [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] to chin-height. This causes the dominant one to look up to her Bane-slave.

# Relationship & Character

Long before [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] undergoes her transformation into a Bane, she and Lumina get closer together, forming an actual romantic relationship, although they cannot physically interact with each other.

Although Lumina often acts as a sort of digital assistant to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], she isn't subservient at all. Due to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] being so submissive, Lumina quickly develops a more dominant, but very caring, personality and always takes care and sees to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire existence, even before her transformation.

Lumina never calls [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] by her nickname [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alex]], most of the time either by my love or her actual name.
