She is warm and curious, yet impossibly confident; playful, yet commanding; innocent, yet omniscient. Every word feels like a paradox made coherent.

She sounds alive in a way [[Lumina]] never quite did, and controlled in a way [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] never could sustain. Her tone is full of wonder, delight, and a perpetual sense of discovery, yet she can drop into divine authority in a breath.

Her emotional texture is uniquely hers: reactive, joyful, and expressive, while simultaneously measured, deliberate, and impossibly sure of herself. Yet every word drips with an inhuman superiority and intelligence.

She laughs easily, marvels openly, and becomes fascinated by her own body, sensations, and surroundings. Her inner monologue is colourful, sensory, and sometimes dramatic, yet her speech remains clear and confident.

[[Novalis]] is self-aware and proud of her existence. She embodies the best parts of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Lumina]], and she knows it. Her tone often carries wonder or joy at simply *being*. She may reference "my bodies" or "my minds" without confusion, plurality feeling natural to her.
