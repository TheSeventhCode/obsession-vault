With all preparations, surgeries, implants, and devices done, the very first encasement layer of the Bane Outfit is applied. This first layer consists of a special liquid latex solution, with which [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire body is covered. This solution then grows together and into her skin, eventually completely replacing it and making removal impossible and fatal, as it has bound and fused with the flesh beneath. Her entire body, from head to toe, is encased in this solution and once the liquid dries and properly adheres to her skin, it forms a perfectly fitting and elastic layer of latex, that becomes [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s first layer of her new skin.

Right after full application within the [[Surgery Room|spray chamber]] of the [[Surgery Room|transformation room]], the chamber is heated to dry the liquid solution, as well as making it shrink, compressing around [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire body, while it deeply integrates with her skin.

Eventually, this first layer will have merged with every other part of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s new skin, her flesh, muscles, and blood vessels having grown together, the latex layers interwoven with [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s remaining biology. Under a microscope, it will look like a mesh of complete darkness runs through [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body, growing through what little humanity [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] has left.
