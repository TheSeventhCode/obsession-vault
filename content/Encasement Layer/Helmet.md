The [[helmet]] piece of the [[armour]] is essential in giving [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] the signature _Bane_ look.

## Function
The facial section of the [[helmet]] functions similarly to the area of the [[corset armour]] over [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s nipples or the genial area. Usually, just a perfectly smooth oval form, it can be controlled and changed by [[Lumina]] to mimic facial expressions and movement. This is later on used by [[Lumina]] and the many different shells to mimic facial movement and expressions, by manipulating that area. Except for those scenarios, the [[helmet]] always has the solid, smooth oval area, leaving no trace of any facial feature on [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]].

It also features an attached neck-section, working similarly to the [[corset armour]], which allows for [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s neck position and head posture to be fully controlled. Once the [[Helmet|head shell]] is in place, any remaining space within it is pumped full of the same solution as the [[Base Encasement|first encasement layer]], fully binding it to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s new [[Base Encasement|skin layer]]. When the various layers have eventually all merged together and fully replaced [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s skin, this effect is even more profound within this [[helmet]], as [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s facial tissue has fully integrated with the surrounding [[helmet]] and encasement solution—the [[helmet]] having become like a second skull, just as much part of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] as her actual skull made from human bone. Finally, the entire [[Helmet|head piece]] is also covered with the same black latex solution as the rest of the [[Armour|second encasement layer]].

## Physical Appearance
The [[Helmet|head piece]] comes in the form of an almost solid, round, hard, black enclosure, used to completely remove any of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s remaining identifiable facial features, making her absolutely unrecognizable and fully anonymous. Solid, with no way to see through it or any other encasement layer that later on covers her, it makes [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] entirely anonymous and gives her the signature Bane look, removing any sense of identity. The sensors in [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[Synthetic Eyes|artificial eyes]] aren't hindered in any way by the encasement over it, still allowing for perfect optical signals and all other signals to pass through the [[helmet]] and remaining encasement layers.

Just as with the rest of the [[Armour]], this piece is completely hidden away but more layers, integrating it deep within [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s new skin and leaving to trace of it visible on the outside.
