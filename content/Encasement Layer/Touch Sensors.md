This fourth layer, on top of the enhancements, comes in the form of a thin web of touch sensors, applied over [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire encased body. Covering every single area, this web makes it possible to forward an increased sensitive artificial sense of touch to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], more focused and detailed than that of a human, all while still under the full control of [[Lumina]].

Even when covered by the [[Outer Encasement Layer|final layer]] of any shells, this web of touch sensors give [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] a superhuman sense of touch. She's able to feel slight changes in air current and can even recognize if a spec of dust lands on her encased body. But all of those signals are under full control by [[Lumina]] and only available to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] if her AI [[Lumina|Mistress]] allows it.

Again, this layer is also encased in another layer of the smooth black latex solution, as the [[armour]] and [[Enhancement Layer|third layer]].
