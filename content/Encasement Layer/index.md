[[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[Encasement Layer/index|Latex Skin]] is the combination of all her encasement layers — her [[base encasement]], [[armour]], [[Corset Armour|corset piece]], [[helmet]], [[Enhancement Layer|synthetic muscles]], [[Touch Sensors|sensory mesh]], and [[outer encasement layer]] — which eventually all merges together into a single, permanent material that has completely replaced [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s human skin.

This special compound fully interweaves these layers, making them impossible to separate from each other or from [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]. Her new [[Encasement Layer/index|latex skin]] having fully merged with her biological flesh, muscles, and blood vessels. It has interwoven itself with [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s remaining biology, the blood vessels and flesh having grown into the foundational latex coating, and [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body having recognized this layer as her actual skin.

In the end, it is a singular compound possessing all the features and functions of the originally separate parts, while being utterly *impossible* to remove from [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s transformed body.
