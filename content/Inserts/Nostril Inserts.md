With no need for her natural airways, surgically removed nose, no ability to even breathe air, long [[Nostril Inserts|nose plugs]] are inserted into [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s sinuses. These plugs extend from the entry of what was once her nose, past her sinuses, all the way into her nasal cavity, reaching the very outset of her throat where they make contact with the massive [[gag]].

## Function
Once fully inserted, each plug is inflated with the [[Biocompatible Polymer]] that solidifies into a permanent structure, effectively filling any remaining voids within [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s nasal passages. This polymer is formulated to expand and fuse intensely with her surrounding tissue, creating a seamless bond that integrates deeply with her nasal cavity, and making them completely irremovable. The space filled by the polymer eventually fuses enough with the surrounding tissue, it will be like there has never been any space for sinuses or nasal airways.

This blockage removes any sense of physical smell for [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], making her dependent for another sense on [[Lumina]]'s simulation through [[Brain Implant]]. Any kind of sense or taste is now completely done through a simulation by the [[Brain Implant|neural connection]].
