With no need for her natural airways, and no ability to even breathe air through her nose, long nose plugs are inserted into [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s nostrils. These plugs extend from the entry of her nose all the way into her nasal cavity, reaching the very outset of her throat where they make contact with the massive [[gag]].

Once fully inserted, each plug is inflated with the [[biocompatible polymer]] that solidifies into a permanent structure, effectively filling any remaining voids within [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s nasal passages. This polymer is formulated to expand and fuse intensely with her surrounding tissue, creating a seamless bond that integrates deeply with her nasal cavity, and making them completely irremovable.

This blockage removes any sense of physical smell for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], making her dependent for another sense on [[Lumina]]'s simulation through [[brain implant]]. Any kind of sense or taste is now completely done through a simulation by the [[Brain Implant|neural connection]].
