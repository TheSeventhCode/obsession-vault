[[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], eventually then [[Lumina]], owns a large rectangular property on which almost all of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s recent and future life happens. It is surrounded by tall and thick bushes, isolating and blocking any view from the outside.

The front, where the large main gate is located, has a broad grove, through which the [[driveway]] snakes itself up to the [[Alexandra's Mansion|mansion]]. The circular [[driveway]] stops right in front of the [[Alexandra's Mansion|mansion]], at the front gate, and serves as a small roundabout with beautiful marble decorations and a large central fountain, with a remarkable life-size statue as its centrepiece at the top.

On the left-hand-side of the [[Alexandra's Mansion|Mansion]], just bordering the circular [[driveway]] and connected to the [[Alexandra's Mansion|mansion]], is a large side-building that acts as a garage for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s various vehicles, such as the [[Alexandra's Car|Lumen]], [[Alexandra's Motorcyle|Luminous]], and [[Transportation Truck|Umbra]]. On the other side, as well as behind the [[Alexandra's Mansion|mansion]], is a long outdoor [[pool]].

Behind the [[Alexandra's Mansion|mansion]] is a large garden. Further down, another soothing grove is located, in which there's also a fully automated greenhouse.
