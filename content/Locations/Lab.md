The [[lab]] is a large clean-room deep within [[Alexandra's mansion]] and on the basement level. In here, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Lumina]] have developed and manufactured all the devices, materials, and systems for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s transformation.

This [[surgery room]] is divided into various sections, one being an even more sterile operating section, in which a fully automated and highly advanced surgical table is located. This operating table allows [[Lumina]] to perform any kind of surgery on [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], impossible for any human surgeon. It includes systems for automated IVs or injections, countless delicate robotic arms to work on the tiniest of surgical tasks, anywhere in [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body.

Another section is the [[Surgery Room|spray chamber]], a large cylinder located on one side of the [[Surgery Room|transformation room]]. It is used to apply the countless permanent layers of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s Bane transformation.

The main area is like a central hub for everything and serves as storage, with various highly organised storage containers. It is later used to store a couple of the instances of the [[Alexandra's Proxy|AlexDroid]] proxy.
