This is a clean room deep within [[Alexandra's mansion]], in which all of her surgeries are performed by [[Lumina]]. The custom automatic operating table is in there, as well as the spray chamber to eventually transform [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] into a Bane.

The spray chamber is a large cylinder at one side of the transformation room. It is used to apply the countless permanent layers for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s Bane transformation.

Later on, this room is also used to store a couple of instances of the [[Alexandra's Proxy|AlexDroid]] proxy.
