In the [[living room]] of [[Alexandra's mansion]], there's a [[Living Room Vacuum Bed|vacuum bed]] made of reinforced black latex. When not in use, it is hidden away in a compartment in the high ceiling of the [[living room]]. Two large hatches open up to lower down the [[Living Room Vacuum Bed|vacuum bed]] from the ceiling. These latches can also close again once the [[Living Room Vacuum Bed|vacuum bed]] is past them, letting it appear as if the suspension wires disappear into the ceiling.

The [[Living Room Vacuum Bed|vacuum bed]] can then be suspended from the high ceiling or automatically be lowered down to the floor, stopping just below hip height.

## Function
Between and within the heavy latex sheets of the [[Living Room Vacuum Bed|vacuum bed]], there's a cable to connect to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[maintenance port]], allowing for full maintenance sessions, while still being completely trapped within.

Once fully sealed, the negative pressure is so strong, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] is completely immobilized between the thick latex sheets. After her Bane transformation, [[Lumina]] decides that this [[Living Room Vacuum Bed|vacuum bed]] also becomes [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s new bed, stating that she would primarily sleep in here most of the time.

The rectangular, heavy black latex that makes up the entire [[Living Room Vacuum Bed|vacbed]] only has one opening at the side. Once [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] is fully inside, that open side is closed off and actually glued shut with a special chemical solution, ensuring that absolutely no air can escape or enter. Only another solvent solution, controlled by the automated systems by [[Lumina]], can open that side again for [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] to be freed.

[[Lumina]] often uses [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] encased in the [[Living Room Vacuum Bed|vacuum bed]] as her own bed, her projection lying on top of the immobilized and encased [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], playing with [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s extreme contours of her slave, or just seemingly sleeping on top of her, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] serving as mattress and cushion.

## Physical Appearance
The [[Living Room Vacuum Bed|vacuum bed]] is made from a rectangular steel frame, as well as two extremely thick and dense black latex sheets, made from a similar material as [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[outer encasement layer]].

The bed can be lowered down by [[Lumina]], hovering at the lowest point at around waist height over the floor, so [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] can enter and connect the cable to the [[maintenance port]]. Once everything is ready, the opening in the bed closes and the air is pumped out, vacuum packing [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s encased form in between the thick layers, causing her absurd figure to press through the thick latex. Finally, the entire structure is lifted into the air again, suspending it 3 metres in the air, with [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] helplessly being trapped within it, as long as [[Lumina]] wishes. The latex bed is strong enough to easily carry [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body, without the slightest risk of it ripping and [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] falling out of it.
