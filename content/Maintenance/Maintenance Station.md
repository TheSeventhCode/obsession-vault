Once [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s transformation into a Bane is complete, the process of supplying her with all the substances she needs is done with the use of maintenance stations. They all connect to the [[maintenance port]] between the legs of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s system in some way and perform the necessary hygiene processes and supply nutritional solution and oxygen into her tanks, as well as water into her stomach.

The maintenance stations all go through the same [[maintenance process]].

## Function
[[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Lumina]] have come up with multiple variants of these cleaning stations:
- The primary one is located in the [[maintenance room]] within [[Alexandra's mansion]]. Most of the system is underneath the floor, with only a long cylindrical pole visible above it, which acts as the connection to the [[maintenance port]] between [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s legs. Once the end of the pole has been connected to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s port, the pole begins to rise, lifting [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] up and causing her full weight to be held by the pole and the gigantic phalluses within her genitals. With [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] fully impaled on the pole, it rises about three metres into the air, positioning [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] perfectly in the centre of the entire [[maintenance room]]. As [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] cannot move away, her latex Bane form is held like a mannequin in position, the pole acting as the stand, her entire weight carried by the genital devices, while she helplessly endures the entire cleaning procedure. This station also has a fourth phase in the [[maintenance process]] in the form of two robot arms, one from the floor, one from the ceiling, surrounding [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] where she is suspended. They then spray an extremely small layer of the [[Outer Encasement Layer|outer skin]] on [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire body, ensuring she never loses her shine or complete black, no matter what happens to her already almost indestructible outer surface.
