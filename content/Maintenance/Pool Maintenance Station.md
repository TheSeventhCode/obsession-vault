The [[pool maintenance station]] is a fully functional [[maintenance station]], built into the floor of the indoor section of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[pool]].

The only visible outside part of it is a cable that can be extended from an almost hidden opening in the floor, only discernable if [[Lumina]] opens the related hatch. The cable connects to the [[maintenance port]] in the back of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s mermaid shell, and allows for full maintenance.
