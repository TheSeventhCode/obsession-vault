Another, far easier to use, version of the [[maintenance station]] comes in the form of a slightly larger suitcase. From the suitcase, a cable can be pulled out, that serves as the connection to the [[maintenance port]]. Manually preparing all the necessary substances, they can then be pumped into [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s systems by the [[Portable Maintenance Station|portable cleaning station]], although it has an integrated air-filtration and compression system to gather the necessary oxygen that is pumped into [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s left breast for [[Alternative Air Supply|air supply]].

This version is far more used during travel, or if any other methods aren't possible, although it requires more frequent use, because of the smaller dose and volume it's only capable of supplying.
