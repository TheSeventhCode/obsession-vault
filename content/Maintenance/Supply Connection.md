To solve [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s nutritional and [[Alternative Air Supply|air supply]], once she's fully transformed into a Bane, a special connection tube is inserted through her entire body. The end of the massive [[anal plug]], that ends at the start of the small intestine, continues on as a smaller cable, travelling through the small intestine and upward, all the way through, into [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s stomach. This end, acts as a connection point for her [[Alternative Air Supply|air tank]] and [[Food Supply|food tank]], as well as attaching itself to the end of the huge phallus of the [[gag]], that comes down from [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s mouth, down her throat, and ends in her stomach. The end in her stomach also has a little balloon attached to it, that, once inflated with a hardening liquid, acts as a stopper, so the supply tube cannot be pulled out through the digestive system any more.
