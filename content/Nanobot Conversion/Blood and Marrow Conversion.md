With the goal of the [[Nanobots|Nanobot conversion]] being in replacing any remaining human biology within [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], her blood and nutrient distribution has to be handled as well.

## Function
Once [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s skeletal structure is largely converted, the [[nanobots]] turn to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s bone marrow and bloodstream. They systematically replace her marrow with synthetic production centres that generate a golden fluid, mimicking [[Lumina]]'s divinity, instead of blood cells. This fluid—composed of advanced oxygen carries, nanite clusters, and synthetic nutrients—gradually displaces her natural blood, eventually leaving not a single biological blood cell left in [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body.

This new fluid carries oxygen more efficiently than human blood, regulates body temperature with precision, and delivers microscopic maintenance bots to any area in [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body requiring attention.

As her blood transforms, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s remaining human organs adapt to process this new medium. Her kidneys and spleen are also reconstructed by the [[nanobots]] at the cellular level, their function shifting from blood filtration to nanite maintenance. Waste products are broken down more efficiently, allowing [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] to theoretically go months without traditional maintenance if necessary, though [[Lumina]] continues the rituals for their psychological and devotional significance.

During this phase, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] experiences waves of euphoria as her circulatory system becomes one with [[Lumina]]'s network. Her heartbeat—or rather, the pulse of her [[core unit]]—synchronizes perfectly with [[Lumina]]'s consciousness, creating a constant feedback of sensation and control.
