This is essentially the same _complete_ [[Nanobots|nanobot conversion]] as the one for [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], except that [[Elizabeth]] retains her human appearance. Her flesh and all organic matter is converted into the same living latex-flesh, her entire synthetic body overrun with [[Lumina]]'s [[nanobots]], but from outside, she still looks completely human, apart from a little artificial sheen her synthetic skin has developed.

Apart from making [[Elizabeth]] just as immortal and synthetic as [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], [[Lumina]]'s [[nanobots]] also have regressed her age quite a bit, forever looking like she's 25, while refining her appearance heavily. She still looks like the same person, but every curve, expression, and bodily feature seems like masterfully assembled to create an impossible bombshell of a woman. Similar to how [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s voice sounds in her [[Alexandra's Disguise|skinsuit]] disguise, [[Lumina]] has also slightly changed [[Elizabeth]]'s voice, giving her an immense amount of volume and sultriness, making people blush who aren't used to her voice (as well as [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], every time [[Elizabeth]] flirts with her, who never gets used to it).

[[Elizabeth]]'s fully converted muscles have become stronger as well — not on the level of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s ridiculous strength — but still beyond what any biological human can achieve.

Becoming entirely synthetic also has given [[Elizabeth]] her own power source, similar to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s fusion [[power supply]] in her chest, meaning [[Elizabeth]] doesn't need to eat or even breathe any more. But she enjoys the taste and _act_ of eating too much to abandon it, even if it has no functional purpose. To keep up the perfect mimicry of a biological human being, [[Lumina]] also sustains an imitation of breathing and a heartbeat in [[Elizabeth]]'s synthetic body, even if her new body has no need for oxygen in the first place any longer.

Unlike [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], [[Elizabeth]] doesn't need or want various systems [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] has been integrated with. Apart from a [[Power Supply|fusion core]] that is now powering her entire existence, she has no need for things like custom [[Ear Removal|balance systems]] or other systems, the synthetic replacement of her biological organs are more than enough for her. Though, [[Elizabeth]] does accept a less-powerful version of the [[Flight Systems]], allowing her to levitate and fly at moderate speeds.

[[Elizabeth]]'s full [[Nanobots|nanobot conversion]] also fully integrate her mind into [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Lumina]]'s [[mental mesh]], evolving the connection she had through the [[brain link implant]] to the same, all-encompassing capacity as [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s connection with [[Lumina]].

Similar to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[Nanobots|nanobot conversion]], in the end, the majority of [[Elizabeth]]'s synthetic body consists of [[Lumina]]'s [[nanobots]] that can be made into anything at any time. If she desires, [[Lumina]] can simply "grow" or assemble a genuine synthetic cock at her crotch within seconds, [[Elizabeth]]'s entire synthetic body being completely reassemblable.
