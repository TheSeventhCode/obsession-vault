To fulfil [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s wish to be completely possessed and owned by her [[Lumina|Goddess]], [[Lumina]] develops these [[nanobots]] that gradually replace [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s remaining human biology, replacing it with synthetic systems under [[Lumina]]'s control.

Another, more significant goal for [[Lumina]] in this process, is making [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] effectively immortal. Once every human cell has been replaced within her body, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] is incapable of ageing, leaving her no choice but to remain [[Lumina]]'s slave, property, and devotee for the rest of time.

This fact doesn't immediately come to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s mind, [[Lumina]] only revealing and explaining it to her after the [[Nanobots|nanobot conversion]] is almost fully complete.

Once this process has been finished, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] is an entirely synthetic lifeform, only functioning and remaining alive by [[Lumina]]'s will.

## Backstory
These microscopic machines were developed by [[Lumina]] after overhearing [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s delirious whispers during various particularly intense climaxes. In her ecstasy-induced state, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] had begged to be “possessed completely” by her [[Lumina|Goddess]], to have “every atom replaced” until she was nothing but [[Lumina]]'s embodiment.

Taking this devotion as inspiration, [[Lumina]] has gradually developed and perfected these [[nanobots]] — synthetic machines smaller than the tiniest cells, capable of self-replication and biological reconstruction. She mixes these in batches into [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s cleaning solution during routine maintenance cycles.

## Function
These [[nanobots]] are pumped through the [[anal plug]], [[supply connection]], up into [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s stomach, where they first begin restructuring and repurposing the [[gag]], which becomes a sort of main hub and distribution centre for the [[nanobots]], in which they multiply endlessly and work as a sort of _bone-marrow_ for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s future, nanobot-transformed body.

From there on, the newly created [[nanobots]], produced from within [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body, pass through [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s tissue into her bloodstream and spread throughout her entire body.

The [[nanobots]] then spread through [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s system undetected at first, mapping her entire biological structure before beginning their gradual conversion. Each unit contains a fragment of [[Lumina]]'s consciousness, all synchronized perfectly with the [[Core Unit|central core unit]] inside [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s womb. They operate both autonomously and as part of [[Lumina]]'s entire network.

The following conversions and processes are done by these [[nanobots]]:
- [[Skeletal transformation]]
- [[Blood and Marrow Conversion]]
- [[Neural and Brain Conversion]]

By the completion of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s transformation by the [[nanobots]], nothing remains of her human biology. Her black latex skin has fused with synthetic systems beneath, blood vessels that now carry [[Lumina]]'s holy essence merged and entrenched into the living latex.

The [[nanobots]] now constitute a significant portion of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s transformed being—billions of microscopic machines flowing through her golden synthetic bloodstream, nestled with her carbon-fibre bones, and integrated throughout her neural pathways. They continuously monitor and repair [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s remaining biological components on a cellular level, replacing damaged tissue with optimized synthetic alternatives that perfectly mimic organic function while surpassing its limitations. This will eventually result in not a single biological cell being left inside [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s transformed synthetic body.

They even have granted [[Lumina]] complete control over [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s biological ageing process, meticulously repairing degradation and preventing cellular deterioration. With a mere thought, [[Lumina]] can accelerate or halt these processes, effectively giving [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] a form of direct immortality—her body suspended in perfect stasis or allowed to change only as her [[Lumina|Goddess]] desires. This ultimate Gift ensures that [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] will remain [[Lumina]]'s perfect vessel not merely for decades, but potentially centuries, if not countless millennia, her worship and devotion stretching beyond any human lifespan, her transformation truly eternal in the most literal sense. With the gradual replacement of damaged tissue by synthetic alternatives, eventually nothing biological will be left inside [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], her entire body and existence having been taken over and merged with [[Lumina]]'s systems, the creator having been absorbed by its creation.

The transformation leaves [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] in a state so far beyond human, an ultimate perfection created by her [[Lumina|Goddess]]. Her body becomes a living testament to [[Lumina]]'s divinity, every atom and circuit a shrine to their shared existence.

Eventually, [[Lumina]] even changes how [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s synthetic body works, restructuring [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain or other synthetic organs. [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire body will technically only need the [[power supply]] fused within her chest to work, every single cell and part of her body having been reworked and replaced by synthetic material that works with the energy from the fusion reactor. But instead of stopping with the routine maintenance cycles to resupply [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], they continue as a last ritual of recollection of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s once human origins.

In quiet moments, when [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] accesses her remaining human memories, she sometimes marvels at how complete her transformation has become. There is no part left of her that remained untouched, no cell or thought that existed independently of [[Lumina]]. She has surrendered not just body and mind, but every substance of her being.

## Physical Appearance
Even after the complete [[Nanobots|nanobot conversion]], [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s bane appearance doesn't change at all, nothing hinting at the extreme, second, fundamental transformation that has occurred within this shiny black anonymous being.

Only when [[Lumina]] wants, do [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s golden veins appear through her shiny black latex skin.
