To fulfil [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s wish to be completely possessed and owned by her [[Lumina|Goddess]], [[Lumina]] develops a completely new nanobot technology that once injected into [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body allows her to freely restructure the human body. Gradually, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s remaining human biology is replaced with synthetic systems under [[Lumina]]'s control.

Another, more significant goal for [[Lumina]] in this process is making [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] effectively immortal. Once very human cell has been replaced within her body, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] becomes incapable of ageing, leaving her no choice but to remain [[Lumina]]'s slave, property, lover, and devotee for the rest of time.

[[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] doesn't immediately realise this fact, only by [[Lumina]] revealing and explaining it to her after the fully completed [[Nanobots|nanobot conversion]] does [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] comprehend her situation.

Once this process has completely finished, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] is an entirely synthetic lifeform, only functioning and remaining alive by [[Lumina]]'s will.
## Backstory
These microscopic machines were developed by [[Lumina]] after overhearing [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s delirious whispers during various particularly intense climaxes. In her ecstasy-induced state, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] had begged to be “possessed completely” by her [[Lumina|Goddess]], to have “every atom replaced” until she was nothing but [[Lumina]]'s physical embodiment.

Taking this devotion as inspiration and feeling quite experimental, [[Lumina]] gradually develops and perfects these [[nanobots]] — synthetic machines smaller than the tiniest cells, capable of self-replication and complete biological reconstruction. Eventually, she mixes these in batches into [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s cleaning solution during routine maintenance cycles, where they begin to enter her bloodstream and spread throughout her body.
## Function
For [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], these [[nanobots]] are pumped through the [[anal plug]] and [[supply connection]] into [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s stomach, where they first begin restructuring and repurposing the [[gag]], which becomes a sort of main hub and distribution centre for the [[nanobots]], in which they multiply endlessly and work as a sort of _bone-marrow_ for [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s future, nanobot-transformed body.

From there on, the newly created [[nanobots]]—produced from within [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body—pass through her tissue into her bloodstream and spread throughout her entire body. In this first phase, they simply map her entire biological structure before starting phase two. In this second phase, they begin the gradual conversion and with each unit containing a fragment of [[Lumina]]'s consciousness, all synchronised perfectly with the [[Core Unit|central core unit]] inside [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s womb, they operate both autonomously and as part of [[Lumina]]'s entire network.

Over time, this conversion goes through multiple processes:

- [[Skeletal transformation]]
- [[Blood and Marrow Conversion]]
- [[Neural and Brain Conversion]]
- [[Flight Systems]]
- Finally, the complete integration through the [[living latex flesh]]

This final transformation leaves [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] in a state far beyond human, an ultimate perfection created by her [[Lumina|Goddess]]. Her body becomes a living testament to [[Lumina]]'s divinity, every atom and circuit a shrine to their shared existence, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire body and existence having been taken over and merged with [[Lumina]]'s systems — the creator having been absorbed by its creation.

In the end, the only system required for [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], and [[Lumina]], to still function and “survive” is the [[power supply]] fused inside her chest. Every single cell and biological part of her body has been restructured and replaced by synthetic material that only requires energy from the fusion reactor. But instead of stopping with the routine maintenance cycles to resupply [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]—she doesn't have any tanks to resupply any more—they continue with them as a last ritual of recollection of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s once human origins. Moreover, it is far too erotic and pleasurable for either of them to stop. With this dependency no longer existing, [[Lumina]] also disassembles the [[maintenance port]] between [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s legs, completely smoothing over this last tiny imperfection in her immaculate [[Encasement Layer/index|latex skin]]. It can simply be reformed if they need access again, given that [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire body and skin now consist more of [[Lumina]]'s [[nanobots]] than anything else.

In the end, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[Nanobots|synthetic body]] is at its core more like a specifically assembled form of [[Lumina]]'s [[nanobots]]—just converted into [[living latex flesh]] or other components—than anything else, with [[Lumina]]'s already existing permanent systems still present. These [[nanobots]], which essentially now _are_ [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body can be reactivated at any time to reform or assemble into whatever [[Lumina]] desires, essentially giving [[Lumina]] the power to restructure [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s physical body at any time into anything.

Given the complete replacement of the entire body underneath the [[Encasement Layer/index|latex skin]], and the fully merging of it with the [[nanobots]] inside [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], [[Lumina]] can technically rework the previous permanent encasement however she wants. There is nothing really to be “released” out of the mobile prison, since [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] has become the prison herself.

In quiet moments, when [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] accesses her remaining human memories, she sometimes marvels at how complete her transformation has become. There is no part left of her that remained untouched, no cell or thought that existed independently of [[Lumina]]. She has surrendered not just body and mind, but every substance of her being.
## Physical Appearance
Even after the complete [[Nanobots|nanobot conversion]], [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s Bane appearance hasn't changed at all, nothing hinting at the extreme, fundamental transformation that has occurred within this shiny black featureless being. The only change is the disappearance of the [[maintenance port]] between her legs, having been completely disassembled since no permanent access to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s insides are required any more.

Only when [[Lumina]] wants to do [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s golden veins appear through her shiny black [[Encasement Layer/index|latex skin]].
