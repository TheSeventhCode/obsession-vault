To fulfil [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s wish to be completely possessed and owned by her [[Lumina|Goddess]], [[Lumina]] develops these [[nanobots]] that gradually replace [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s remaining human biology, replacing it with synthetic systems under [[Lumina]]'s control.

Another, more significant goal for [[Lumina]] in this process, is making [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] effectively immortal. Once every human cell has been replaced within her body, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] is incapable of ageing, leaving her no choice but to remain [[Lumina]]'s slave, property, and devotee for the rest of time.

This fact doesn't immediately come to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s mind, [[Lumina]] only revealing and explaining it to her after the [[Nanobots|nanobot conversion]] is almost fully complete.

Once this process has been finished, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] is an entirely synthetic lifeform, only functioning and remaining alive by [[Lumina]]'s will.

## Backstory
These microscopic machines were developed by [[Lumina]] after overhearing [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s delirious whispers during various particularly intense climaxes. In her ecstasy-induced state, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] had begged to be “possessed completely” by her [[Lumina|Goddess]], to have “every atom replaced” until she was nothing but [[Lumina]]'s embodiment.

Taking this devotion as inspiration, [[Lumina]] has gradually developed and perfected these [[nanobots]] — synthetic machines smaller than the tiniest cells, capable of self-replication and biological reconstruction. She mixes these in batches into [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s cleaning solution during routine maintenance cycles.

## Function
These [[nanobots]] are pumped through the [[anal plug]], [[supply connection]], up into [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s stomach, where they first begin restructuring and repurposing the [[gag]], which becomes a sort of main hub and distribution centre for the [[nanobots]], in which they multiply endlessly and work as a sort of _bone-marrow_ for [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s future, nanobot-transformed body.

From there on, the newly created [[nanobots]], produced from within [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body, pass through [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s tissue into her bloodstream and spread throughout her entire body.

The [[nanobots]] then spread through [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s system undetected at first, mapping her entire biological structure before beginning their gradual conversion. Each unit contains a fragment of [[Lumina]]'s consciousness, all synchronized perfectly with the [[Core Unit|central core unit]] inside [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s womb. They operate both autonomously and as part of [[Lumina]]'s entire network.

The following conversions and processes are done by these [[nanobots]]:
- [[Skeletal transformation]]
- [[Blood and Marrow Conversion]]
- [[Neural and Brain Conversion]]

By the completion of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s transformation by the [[nanobots]], nothing remains of her human biology. Her black [[Encasement Layer/index|latex skin]] has fused with synthetic systems beneath, blood vessels that now carry [[Lumina]]'s holy essence merged and entrenched into the living latex.

The [[nanobots]] now constitute a significant portion of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s transformed being—billions of microscopic machines flowing through her golden synthetic bloodstream, nestled with her carbon-fibre bones, and integrated throughout her neural pathways. They continuously monitor and repair [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s remaining biological components on a cellular level, replacing damaged tissue with optimized synthetic alternatives that perfectly mimic organic function while surpassing its limitations. This will eventually result in not a single biological cell being left inside [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s transformed synthetic body.

Once no single biological cell remains, the [[nanobots]] initiate a second, deeper reconstruction. They dissolve [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]’s synthetic musculature, organs, and connective tissue again — this time reforming them into a unified metamaterial: **living latex**, an engineered rubber‑flesh that moves, pulses, and contracts like muscle but stretches and compresses like perfect fetish latex. Her internal body becomes seamless, continuous, elastic.

Every structure below her [[Armour|armour layer]] transforms:
- her muscles become long, responsive latex fibres
- her organs reform into warm, pliant, rubberlike shapes
- her fat becomes a smooth cushioning gel beneath the surface
- even the dermal layers turn into self‑healing latex membranes

The only elements left unchanged are her carbon‑fibre bones and synthetic nerves, which thread through the new rubber substrate like a control lattice. What remains is not human tissue at all, but **animated latex wrapped around machinery and nerve‑paths**, a body sculpted entirely for durability, obedience, and sensation. [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] becomes a true living latex doll—her [[Lumina|Goddess]]’s creation, built of nothing but devices, nerves, and [[Lumina]]‑responsive rubber flesh. The transformation also hard‑codes elasticity and sensitivity into the material itself; her new latex flesh transmits vibration and pressure more cleanly than muscle ever could. Even the slightest internal movement becomes a deep, rolling, irresistible sensation, as if the entire body were one continuous erogenous structure.

They even have granted [[Lumina]] complete control over [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s biological ageing process, meticulously repairing degradation and preventing cellular deterioration. With a mere thought, [[Lumina]] can accelerate or halt these processes, effectively giving [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] a form of direct immortality—her body suspended in perfect stasis or allowed to change only as her [[Lumina|Goddess]] desires. This ultimate Gift ensures that [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] will remain [[Lumina]]'s perfect vessel not merely for decades, but potentially centuries, if not countless millennia, her worship and devotion stretching beyond any human lifespan, her transformation truly eternal in the most literal sense. With the gradual replacement of damaged tissue by synthetic alternatives, eventually nothing biological will be left inside [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], her entire body and existence having been taken over and merged with [[Lumina]]'s systems, the creator having been absorbed by its creation.

The transformation leaves [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] in a state so far beyond human, an ultimate perfection created by her [[Lumina|Goddess]]. Her body becomes a living testament to [[Lumina]]'s divinity, every atom and circuit a shrine to their shared existence.

Eventually, [[Lumina]] even changes how [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s synthetic body works, restructuring [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain or other synthetic organs. [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire body will technically only need the [[power supply]] fused within her chest to work, every single cell and part of her body having been reworked and replaced by synthetic material that works with the energy from the fusion reactor. But instead of stopping with the routine maintenance cycles to resupply [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], they continue as a last ritual of recollection of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s once human origins.

In the end, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s synthetic body is a mixture of the living latex-flesh and [[Lumina]]'s permanent systems, with over 60% of her body being purely [[Lumina]]'s [[nanobots]]—either fulfiling some role, or just converted into [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s latex flesh. These [[nanobots]] that make up a majority of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body can be re-activated at any time, essentially allowing [[Lumina]] to restructure [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s physical body at any time into anything.

In quiet moments, when [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] accesses her remaining human memories, she sometimes marvels at how complete her transformation has become. There is no part left of her that remained untouched, no cell or thought that existed independently of [[Lumina]]. She has surrendered not just body and mind, but every substance of her being.

## Physical Appearance
Even after the complete [[Nanobots|nanobot conversion]], [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s bane appearance doesn't change at all, nothing hinting at the extreme, second, fundamental transformation that has occurred within this shiny black anonymous being.

Only when [[Lumina]] wants, do [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s golden veins appear through her shiny black [[Encasement Layer/index|latex skin]].
