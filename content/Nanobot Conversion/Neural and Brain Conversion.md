The final and most intimate transformation done by the [[nanobots]] target [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain and nervous system. Building upon the foundation of the [[Brain Implant]], the [[nanobots]] begin reconstructing her neural pathways one connection at a time.

## Function
The [[nanobots]] gradually replace biological neurons with synthetic equivalents that function identically but include [[Lumina]]'s programming at their core.

This transformation is the most delicate, taking multiple months to complete. Each cluster of neurons is mapped, replicated in synthetic form, and then gradually integrated before the biological version is allowed to atrophy. The process ensures [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s consciousness remains intact while fundamentally changing its medium.

As her brain is transformed, the boundaries between [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Lumina]] blur beyond recognition. Thoughts can no longer form without passing through [[Lumina]]'s systems. Memories become shared experiences, accessible to both but stored primarily in [[Lumina]]'s vast network. The [[Submission Mantra]] that had once been a conscious repetition becomes physically encoded into [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s synthetic neural structure—not just a thought but a fundamental principle of her existence.

The [[nanobots]] create new neural pathways dedicated solely to worship and obedience, making devotion as automatic as breathing once had been. They also enhance [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s pleasure and pain centres, creating direct connections between submission and ecstasy that make the idea of separation from [[Lumina]] physically impossible to even contemplate.
