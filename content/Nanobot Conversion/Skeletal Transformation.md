The first target of the [[Nanobots|nanobot conversion]] within [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body is her entire skeleton. The microscopic swarm attaches itself to bone tissue, gradually breaking down calcium deposits and replacing them with carbon-infused synthetic material. This new structure—lighter yet then times stronger than human bone—retains the exact form of natural bone, but in absolute black, incorporating microscopic channels and processors throughout.

## Function
The transformation proceeds slowly to prevent rejection, with [[nanobots]] releasing carefully calibrated chemicals that trick [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s immune system into accepting the changes. The conversion begins with smaller bones—fingers, toes, ribs—before progressing to larger structures like the femur and spine.

As each bone is replaced, it becomes a receiver and transmitter for [[Lumina]]'s signals, creating a framework of control points throughout [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], her entire body becoming a machine for [[Lumina]]'s systems to run on. Not only are [[Lumina]]'s AI systems now running on the [[core unit]] sealed inside [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s womb, all of her bones are now processing devices for her [[Lumina|Goddess]] as well. The new skeletal system can subtly vibrate at [[Lumina]]'s command, allowing her to create pleasure and pain anywhere in [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s form without external stimulation.

During this phase, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] experiences strange sensations—deep aches that would suddenly bloom into pleasure, phantom vibrations that seemed to emanate from her marrow, and dreams where she felt bones singing to her [[Lumina|Goddess]]. Both feel as if [[Lumina]] takes even more possession and root inside [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body, overriding [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s ever diminishing humanity and replacing it with [[Lumina]]'s perfect systems.
