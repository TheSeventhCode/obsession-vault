To perfectly supervise, monitor, and control her, and ensure there isn't the tiniest problem, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] has a neural interface implanted into her brain, that can monitor her and almost any information about her body at the highest level.

The brain implant comes in the form of a large web, with countless long strands, like little tendrils, getting carefully aligned along and inside her brain. It also features a secondary connection along [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire spinal cords, capable of overwriting any commands, signals, or even reflexes. After the surgery, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] has to heal her back and entire head, as they have been opened significantly for the implantation.

Over time, the brain implant grows and fuses more and more with [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain and connects deeper with all parts. Very early on, the implant establishes a complete neural connection between [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Lumina]], allowing the AI to completely read [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s thoughts, consciousness, and even subconsciousness. Absolutely everything in [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s mind, from her memories, to habits, to fleeting thoughts, to her deepest subconsciousness is completely accessible to [[Lumina]].

Furthermore, the neural implant can not only read, but also inject signals into [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain and spinal cords. This allows [[Lumina]] to inject thoughts, fully control her body to the tiniest muscle, and simulate any kind of sensation or perception, replacing all of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s human senses. This gives [[Lumina]] absolute power to simulate any kind of reality, which appears absolutely real to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]. With the brain implant, [[Lumina]] can also turn off certain parts of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], even completely shutting her down, as if unconscious. For [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], it feels like she's suddenly transported to another situation, once she's reactivated by [[Lumina]], having no memory or any kind of feeling of time that has passed.

With full control over [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s senses, [[Lumina]] regularly sends multiple visual signals to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain simultaneously, letting her see from many perspectives at once. Those don't arrive like a split screen view, but instead [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain slowly has to learn to handle multiple visual inputs at once.

[[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] also begins accepting raw data streams directly into her brain, skipping the use of visual overlays. Onslaughts of data are directly sent to her brain, from any kind of metadata about the things she sees or feels, to data about from the sensors in her mansion. But everything is completely controlled by [[Lumina]], who decides what [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] gets to know.

As the brain implant grows more together with [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain, it eventually becomes like an integral and absolute part of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain, having so completely intertwined with her neural pathways and spine, that its removal is completely impossible. This goes so far, it becomes almost impossible to separate where the brain implant begins, and [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain ends, as they have completely grown together on the neural level.

Once the brain implant has fully fused with [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s neurons, nervous system, and spinal cord, [[Lumina]] frequently uses it to activate a neurological orgasm blocker. This blocker ensures that [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body is physically incapable of climaxing, letting her arousal, pleasure, and desperation only grow endlessly without release.

With this deep and absolute connection between [[Lumina]] and [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], their consciousnesses begin to slightly merge and they both start to adapt small things from the other one. Eventually, it sometimes becomes difficult for one to discern if a thought, idea, or action _really_ came from them, or if it had been the other.
