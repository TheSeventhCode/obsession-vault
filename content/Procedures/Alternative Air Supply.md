Due to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] getting fully encased within multiply layers of airtight latex, and her throat and mouth being completely filled by the massive [[gag]], a new method for her to receive oxygen is necessary.
## Function
The massive space created within [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s breasts after the removal of the large [[Figure Enhancements|breast implants]] is partially repurposed to meet her oxygen requirements. The implant in her left breast is replaced with a tank containing a nearly pure oxygen solution, with a density similar to the nutrient solution found in the [[Food Supply|food tank]] in her other breast. The tank is surrounded by enough silicone material to provide [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] with the exact same sensation—an identical feeling of touch and behaviour—as if she still had her silicone implants. It is connected to the [[Heart Replacement|artificial heart]], which then feeds the oxygen straight into her bloodstream.

Because of the [[Chemical Engine|CO₂ recycling]] in the [[chemical engine]], much of the injected oxygen is actually regained. This means the [[Alternative Air Supply|oxygen tank]] only needs to be refilled every few months, although [[Lumina]] prefers to make the [[maintenance process]] a much more frequent occurrence.

The resupply connection to the tank is passed in between [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s ribs during her surgery, and is then surgically fed into [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s stomach. This way, the [[Alternative Air Supply|oxygen tank]] in her breast can be resupplied through the [[Supply Connection|supply tube]] going through her entire body.

With [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s lungs completely removed and the [[power supply]] having taken up their place, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] is incapable to breathe any more. Her oxygen is directly fed into her blood stream. Because of this, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s chest doesn't have the usual up and down movements usually associated with breath.
