As [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s throat and mouth is completely filled by the massive [[gag]], and her entire body encased airtight, a new method for her to receive oxygen is necessary.

The space that has been created within [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s massive breasts after the [[Figure Enhancements|breast implants]] are removed, is then partially used for her oxygen requirement. The implant within her left breast is replaced with a tank of nearly pure oxygen. The tank is surrounded with enough silicone material to still provide the exact same sensation for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], an identical feeling of touch and behaviour, as if she still had her silicone implants. It is connected to the [[Heart Replacement|artificial heart]], which then feeds the oxygen straight into her bloodstream.

Because of the CO2 recycling in the [[Heart Replacement|artificial heart]], a lot of the injected oxygen is actually regained. This means that the oxygen tank only really has to be refilled only every few months, although [[Lumina]] likes to make the [[maintenance process]] a far more frequent occurrence.

The resupply connection to the tank is passed in between [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s ribs during her surgery, and is then surgically fed into [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s stomach. This way, the oxygen tank in her breast can be resupplied through the [[Supply Connection|supply tube]] going through her entire body.

With [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s lungs completely removed and the [[power supply]] having taken up their place, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] cannot breathe any more. Her oxygen is directly fed into her blood stream. Because of this, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s chest doesn't have the usual up and down movements usually associated with breath.
