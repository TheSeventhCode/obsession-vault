Due to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s heavy ballet practice in her childhood and teens, and her continued admiration for the discipline, look, movement, and posture of a ballerina, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] had her tendons surgically shortened by [[Lumina]] to modify her feet into a permanently fixed on-point position.
## Physical Appearance
The altered feet result in a completely straight line from her shin to her toes, forcing her to balance entirely on the tips of her toes. Furthermore, much of her heels and parts of her feet had been removed, causing her legs to become a smooth rod, almost needle-like, from her knees down. The protrusion of her heels and the individuality of her toes were completely gone, her feet being shaped into long smooth cones.

She now has to walk on her toes at all times. Later, her entire feet will be fixed into position to further straighten her toes, resulting in her only contact with the floor being the tip of her large toe, as even her toes stand vertically.

Once the entire transformation into a Bane is complete, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] only walks on the tip of her latex toes, making absolutely no sound when walking, the material of her [[Outer Encasement Layer|outer skin]] layer muting any and all sounds. With the transition of her legs to her feet hidden by latex, her legs become like long poles with needle-point endings, without bend from the knees down.
