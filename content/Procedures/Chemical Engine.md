This is a slightly smaller device than the [[Heart Replacement|artificial heart]]. It is situated directly under the [[Heart Replacement|artificial heart]], connected to it, and receives the extracted CO₂ from [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s bloodstream. It uses energy from [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[power supply]] and water to perform a process similar to photosynthesis, turning the CO₂ and water back into pure oxygen and glucose. The glucose is then directed to the [[Food Supply|food tank]], and the oxygen is sent back into the [[Alternative Air Supply|air tank]].

Due to the energy requirements and resulting heat, only a portion of the extracted CO₂ can be recycled. The remainder must be expelled and excreted during the [[maintenance process]]. This also means [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[Alternative Air Supply|oxygen tank]] must be regularly refilled, preventing the entire system from being completely self-sufficient.
