As all of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s senses are fully simulated and injected by [[Lumina]] through the [[brain implant]], [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] has no need for her biological sense of hearing or balance.

[[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s eardrums and inner ear are surgically removed, making it physically impossible for her to hear anything, except through the signals from the [[brain implant]]. Her inner ear is replaced with sensors that give her the same, even more detailed, information, but fully under the control of [[Lumina]]. This allows for even [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s sense of balance and spatial awareness to be controlled completely by [[Lumina]].

Finally, the space within her ear and ear-canal is then filled with an advanced [[biocompatible polymer]] that solidifies into a rigid structure, creating a permanent seal. This material blocks any natural auditory sensation and even integrates with the tissue surrounding the ear, resulting in the entire area growing closed, as if [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] never even had ears.

Finally, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire outer ears are removed, leaving behind only smooth skin, as if [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s human body never had ears to begin with.
