To provide [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] with her required nutrition, once she's fully transformed into a Bane, the emptied out space within her right breast is used for a sort of [[Food Supply|food tank]], storing extremely a high-concentrated liquid [[Food Supply|nutritional supply]].
## Function
Connecting to the end of the [[Supply Connection|supply tube]] in her stomach, similar to the [[Alternative Air Supply|air tank]] within her left breast, this tank can easily be resupplied through the [[Supply Connection|feeding connection]] travelling through [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire body.

The nutritional solution has enough concentration and the tank is large enough for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] to last about a month, although she has to be supplied with water at more regular intervals, the water being needed not just by her own biology, but also the [[chemical engine]] to recycle CO₂. The regained glucose from the [[chemical engine]] is also fed back into this [[food supply]] tank, which is then mixed with the existing nutritional solution.

The extreme implants in her buttocks and hips are also replaced, and special filtration devices are instead placed within the that space. They allow for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s waste to be filtered and partially recycled into more nutrition and water, extending her required time for maintenance. The connection tubes to these filtration devices end within [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s rectum and are later on connected to the [[anal plug]], to allow proper connection to the entire system.

Fully controlled by [[Lumina]], the tank can then drain its [[Food Supply|nutritional supply]] into [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body. The device has numerous little connections into [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s bloodstream, allowing for certain elements to be directly fed into the bloodstream.
