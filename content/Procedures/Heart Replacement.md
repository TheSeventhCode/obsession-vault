To reduce any health risks, problems, and absolutely entwine her with the Bane systems, as well as to surrender even more of her humanity to her new existence, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s heart is removed and replaced with an artificial device. The device is under absolute control by [[Lumina]], and it performs as a perfect heart, but also injects the oxygen from [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[Alternative Air Supply|air tank]] into her blood. The remaining CO2 coming in from the bloodstream is removed and with the combination of water is made into Glucose, as well as a little more oxygen, similar to how photosynthesis works. This way, all the CO2 from [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body is recycled into oxygen and sent into her bloodstream again.

This artificial heart significantly symbolizes how [[Lumina]] has become the very core of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s existence, as the AI is now embedded within [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s womb, and has quite literally become her heart.

The artificial heart is a separate device from the [[power supply]], as it only acts as a combination of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s heart and lungs.

**The synthetic heart pumps differently than a human one, and instead results in a constant stream, instead of pulses. This leaves a very odd sensation, as no pulse of her blood is actually present any more in [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body. With the combination of no pulse, and not breathing, as well as being unable to make any sounds with her missing vocal cords and sealed nose, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] becomes a completely silent and still Bane. [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] has no pulse.**
