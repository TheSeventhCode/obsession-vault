To reduce any health risks, possible health problems, and absolutely entwine her with the Bane systems, as well as to surrender even more of her humanity to her new existence to [[Lumina]], [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s heart is removed and replaced with an artificial device.
## Function
The device is under full control by [[Lumina]], unable to function without her, and it performs as a perfect heart, but also injects the oxygen from [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[Alternative Air Supply|air tank]] into her blood. The remaining CO2 coming in from the bloodstream is removed and sent to the attached [[chemical engine]] below this [[Heart Replacement|artificial heart]], where it is recycled into oxygen and glucose.

This [[Heart Replacement|artificial heart]] profoundly symbolizes how [[Lumina]] has become the very core of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s existence, as the AI is now embedded within [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s womb, and has quite literally become her heart that only functions through [[Lumina]]'s systems.

The [[Heart Replacement|synthetic heart]] pumps differently than a human one. It pumps in a steady, constant stream, instead of pulses, leaving [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] without even the tiniest hint of a human heartbeat. This leaves a very odd sensation, as no pulse of her blood is actually present any more in [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body. With the combination of no pulse, and not breathing, as well as being unable to make any sounds with her missing vocal cords and sealed nose, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] becomes a completely silent and still Bane.
