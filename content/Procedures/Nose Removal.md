Due to having no need for air or sense of smell after her transformation into a Bane, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s nose is surgically removed. The parts inside her head of her sinuses are filled with the [[nostril inserts]], with the entire outer part being removed and smoothed over with synthetic skin.
## Physical Appearance
This final surgical change, completes [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s extreme dehumanization before her encasement, as her entire face has basically been removed, leaving only black orbs for eyes. The entire protrusion of her nose has been removed.
