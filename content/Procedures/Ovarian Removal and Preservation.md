As an essential part of eliminating all health risks from her body before her Bane transformation, and fully integrate with the [[core unit]], [[Lumina]] removes [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s reproductive systems. This procedure involves the complete surgical removal of both [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s ovaries, ensuring there is no tissue left that could cause hormonal or physiological complications post-transformation.

However, [[Lumina]] takes extra measures beyond mere removal. Not wanting “discard” anything useful or precious to either [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] or her, [[Lumina]] delicately harvests the ovaries and unfertilised eggs within them during the procedure. Utilizing a specially made cryogenic preservation technique, these biological materials are promptly flash-frozen in a sterile bio-storage system, locked away deep within the [[Alexandra's Mansion|mansion]].

But [[Lumina]] doesn't actually let [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] know she has preserved them. Even [[Lumina]] herself doesn't believe there is really any use for [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s reproductive systems after her permanent transformation, yet she's unwilling to discard them for some reason.
