The [[power supply]] is a specialized version of the miniature fusion reactor, that is surgically implanted into [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], taking up the space of her lungs. Her lungs are fully removed, as well as her windpipe, vocal cords, and larynx with the [[vocal cords removal]]. The removal of all of this, makes [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] completely mute, unable to create even the slightest sound, due to the missing vocal cords and windpipe.
## Function
This [[Power Supply|fusion core]] supplies all the devices and systems within [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] and which are part of her Bane system with power. As the collection of all the devices doesn't require a lot of energy, and it being a fusion reactor, it has enough fuel given on its initial implantation for over 10 millennia of runtime for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]].

It fully fills out her chest cavity, tightly integrating with her remaining biology, the internal [[Heart Replacement|artificial heart]], and the [[chemical engine]] below.
