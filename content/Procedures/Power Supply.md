The power supply is a specialized version of the miniature fusion reactor, that is surgically implanted into [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], replacing the entire space of her lungs. It also removes her windpipe, vocal cords, and larynx with the [[vocal cords removal]]. The removal of all of this, makes [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] completely mute, unable to create even the slightest sound, due to the missing vocal cords and windpipe.

It supplies all the devices and systems within [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] and which are part of her Bane system with power. As the collection of all the devices doesn't require a lot of energy, and it being a fusion reactor, it has enough fuel given on its initial implantation for over 10 millennia of runtime for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]].
