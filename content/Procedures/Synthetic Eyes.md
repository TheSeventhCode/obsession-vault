Due to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s full Bane encasement leaving absolutely no opening and sealing her airtight, her natural sight will no longer work, posing a possible health risk, and is therefore no longer needed, as it will be fully simulated with the [[Brain Implant]] by [[Lumina]].
## Function
Because of that, and wanting to remove any possible health issues or unnecessary biological systems, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s eyes and eyelids are surgically removed. Into her empty eye-sockets, two orbs are inserted, that have all kinds of visual sensors built in. From Infrared, Ultrasonic, Lidar, Radar, and even a Geiger Counter, as well as a sensor for magnetic fields. The orbs themselves look like black spheres from the outside, before [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s face is encased.

All of these spectra activate and feed data simultaneously and continuously, with no focal point, no depth-of-field adjustment, and no selective attention**.** [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s visual cortex receives overwhelming detail at equal intensity across her entire visual field—every microscopic imperfection, every thermal gradient, every electromagnetic fluctuation rendered with merciless precision all at once.

The [[synthetic eyes]] do not function independently. Their output feeds directly into the [[Brain Implant|neural implant]], which then channels the data through [[Lumina]] before presenting it to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s consciousness. She is functionally blind to anything [[Lumina]] chooses not to show her, yet hyperaware of everything [[Lumina]] decides to reveal.

To be consistent with the simulation from the [[brain implant]], [[Lumina]] injects her own presence into the data of these sensors as well. As an example, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s new Lidar view would still include her, even if [[Lumina]] doesn't actually physically exist, due to being a projection by the [[Brain Implant|neural implant]].

The [[Helmet]] that, with the other sprayed on encasement layers, will be covering [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s head and embedded into the layers, is specifically made to allow for all these sensors to pass through it, without causing any disturbance. With this, and through the [[brain implant]], [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] can see in far more ways than just optical, once she has finished her transformation. But her new senses are fully dependent and under full control of [[Lumina]] to properly pass and translate them for her brain.
