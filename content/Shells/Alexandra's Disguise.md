[[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s disguise is a special shell that [[Lumina]] has created. It is a full-body suit that covers [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire Bane form, made from a skin-like silicone material.

Once fully worn, it makes [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] appear almost human again. With a perfect wig nicely glued to the scalp of it, and the skin tightening nicely all over [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] appears almost like before her entire transformation.

To allow for proper facial movement and imitation, as well as accurate other bodily details, the [[helmet]], nipple, and genital sections of the [[armour]] in [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s skin soften and pass through the details from beneath. With the careful movements of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[helmet]], [[Lumina]] is able to mimic facial movements on the mask that showed [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s old face.

The feet of the Skinsuit are artificial and contain slots for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[Ballet Feet|needle-point feet]] to fit into. This creates the appearance of normal flat feet, although [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s real feet are vertically hidden inside the shins. This causes [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] to gain a tiny amount of height, due to the artificial feet covers for her pointy legs.

To really finish the disguise, the stretchy mouth section of the Skinsuit is glued to the latex of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[Outer Encasement Layer|outer skin]] underneath. This ensures the Skinsuit cannot be removed, without applying the chemical solvent [[Lumina]] has created. The same process is used to glue a variety of wigs to the bald scalp of the Skinsuit when [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] is fully wearing it, achieving a perfect natural hair look and behaviour.

Most of the time, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] uses a long platinum blonde wavy wig, that most resembles what her hair used to be when she had been human, before her permanent [[hair removal]]. The hair of the wig reaches all the way down to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s buttocks, and spreads wide, often covering her entire back.

With all of those features, this disguise lets [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] move and act like a human again. Although her mouth cannot open that much, as it instantly shows the smooth, shiny latex of her true self underneath. With various tiny speakers placed perfectly within the same area, and [[Lumina]] simulating [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s once human voice, it even allows [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] to speak in her human disguise. When in her Skinsuit, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] always talks like a human with her artificial mouth and voice from the Skinsuit, and never through the mansion's speakers.

To allow [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] to keep up appearances in public, the mouth section of the Skinsuit has a liquid collection system that routes any liquid through tubes inside the suit down to the [[maintenance port]] between her legs. This way, she can appear like if she was drinking something, with all the motion replicated perfectly, with the liquid disappearing into her mouth and travelling down inside the suit, to eventually be pumped into the outer balloon of the [[Core Unit|control core unit]] inside her womb for storage until the next [[Maintenance Process|maintenance cycle]].

Very rarely, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Lumina]] inhabit the body at the same time, causing the iris of one of the perfect eye replications to turn golden. Furthermore, the voice that comes out of the artificial mouth and the many speakers inside, are [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Lumina]]'s at the same time, resulting in a dual-voice and mix of the two. This merge only happens if they are alone or only with people that know of [[Lumina]] and [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s true nature, and are aware that [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] is only wearing a human disguise with her Skinsuit.
