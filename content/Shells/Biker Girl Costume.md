This is a little costume for [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] so that she can go out in public, specifically riding her motorcycle.
## Function
- Before getting dressed in anything, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] first is encased in her full [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]] disguise, ensuring she at least appears human under the motorcycle gear.
- [[Lumina]] has created a custom-made motorcycle black racing suit, that fits perfectly [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s extreme figure, fully encasing [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] skin-tight up to her neck. It has an opening in the back that goes from the lower back, up to the neck, and to close the suit, it is sewn closed with steel wire, the ends welded together at the top. Once closed, the opening basically disappears, leaving no hint of how the suit is actually put on.
    - The suit comes with integrated protectors over the entire body—even though they aren't really necessary given [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s absolute protection by her [[armour]]—as well as integrated motorcycle boots and gloves. All these protections are fully functional and exceed what any standard [[Biker Girl Costume|motorcycle suit]] is capable of, although [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] doesn't actually need any of these protections. Her new [[Encasement Layer/index|latex skin]] alone as a Bane is so completely impenetrable and impossible to damage, no crash could ever hurt or pierce her Bane skin. In the end, the protectors of the suit are basically just for aesthetic purposes.
    - To allow for full maintenance and connection to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s motorcycle, the suit also has a connection port between the legs. This allows a connection to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s actual [[maintenance port]] even when wearing the suit, making a full [[Maintenance Process|maintenance cycle]] possible, as well as integrating with her bike, it can actually be powered.
- From the outside, a standard motorcycle [[helmet]] is then added. At the neck area, this [[helmet]] actually connects to the endings of the welded closed steel wire of the suit. These endings are inserted into a small opening at the back of the [[helmet]], which locks onto these endings, connecting the [[helmet]] to the suit and locking it on [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] as well. Under the chin, the [[helmet]] also has a strap, that actually hides some steel wire again, that once closed to the other side is welded closed, ensuring the [[helmet]] cannot be removed.
    - The black motorcycle [[helmet]] comes with an opaque black visor, making it impossible to see [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s disguised face.

With all connections welded shut, the entire [[Biker Girl Costume|motorcycle outfit]] cannot be removed without [[Lumina]] explicitly helping to open the welded wires back in the [[Alexandra's Mansion|mansion]].
## Physical Appearance
Once fully put on, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] almost looks like a more sporty biker girl with some premium gear, if it wasn't for her absurdly [[Figure Enhancements|extreme proportions]] — her figure being the only thing she can never hide or disguise.
