This is a costume [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]] that [[Lumina]] has created for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] to perfectly cosplay an extremely obscene version of Barbie. With [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[Figure Enhancements|extreme proportions]], even the perfect disguise cannot hide the impossibly giant breasts, miniscule waist, and huge butt.

With a perfect wig and a collection of extremely kitschy clothes, [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] becomes a perfect bimbo version of Barbie. Importantly, all of these clothes are created in a specific manner that makes it possible for them to be either locked or sewn onto [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s disguised body, ensuring that without [[Lumina]]'s explicit permission and help, she cannot remove any of them.

To make the act work even more, [[Lumina]] makes temporary changes to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s mental state, behaviour, and personality, so she also acts as a kitschy, fashion obsessed Barbie.
