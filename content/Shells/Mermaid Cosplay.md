The Mermaid Cosplay [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]] is a full-body [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]] designed to transform [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] into the perfect, traditional mermaid version of herself, complete with a shiny scaly tail and enhanced features.

### **Material and Appearance**

The suit covers [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire Bane form in a skin-like material, combined with the appearance of shimmering scales from the waist down. The scales are an iridescent mix of blues, greens, and purples, mimicking the natural, magical appearance of a mermaid's tail.

From the waist up, the suit is fabricated to resemble smooth, human skin, with strategically placed seashell accents covering the chest area. The material is soft yet durable, ensuring comfort and flexibility while maintaining a realistic look.

### **Entry and Sealant Method**

The Mermaid Cosplay [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]] adopts the same entry and sealant method as [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s regular [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]]. [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] enters the suit through the stretchable mouth section, until she has completely encased herself. The mouth section is then glued to the Bane skin underneath using a special spray-on solution created by [[Lumina]]. This solution ensures a seamless and secure fit, and it is only removable with a particular chemical solvent.

### **Face and Hair Customization**

For the face, a [[helmet]]-like structure covers [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s Bane face, allowing for proper facial mimicry. The facial section of the suit is designed to pass through and replicate [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s old facial features, complete with softening areas for accurate mouth, eye, and other facial movements.

To complete the mermaid look, the suit includes a long, luxurious wig, exactly like [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s old dark red colour, reaching down to her lower back. The wig is glued securely to the scalp section of the [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]], ensuring it remains in place during underwater activities.

### **Feet and Tail Structure**

The lower half of the suit features an artificial mermaid tail that begins at the waist and extends down to her feet. The tail includes the same port for her pointy legs to fit into securely, ensuring a realistic appearance of a continuous, undivided tail. The tail has built-in, horizontally aligned flippers at the end to facilitate swimming movements.

As with the regular [[Latex Mermaid Tail]], the [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]] has a [[maintenance port]] between [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s legs, integrated seamlessly into the design, making it completely hidden when unneeded. This port forwards connections to the [[genital cover]] inside, maintaining the functionalities for fluid collections.

### **Mouth and Voice Features**

The mouth section of the [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]] includes a liquid collection system for any fluids [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] “drinks” while in her disguise. The liquid travels through internal tubes down to the [[maintenance port]], temporarily storing fluids in the outer balloon of the [[Core Unit|control core unit]] inside her womb.

With embedded tiny speakers, the [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]] replicates [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s voice in her mermaid form, allowing her to speak naturally. When [[Lumina]] participates in occupancy, the eye's iris may turn gold, and the dual-voice system activates, combining both voices for a unique, harmonious effect.

### **Mobility and Storage**

The suit allows [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] to swim efficiently while fully disguised. For out-of-water movement, a specially designed chaise longue wheelchair is available. It's controlled by [[Lumina]] and tailored to accommodate the mermaid tail, ensuring easy navigation within the mansion and its premises.
