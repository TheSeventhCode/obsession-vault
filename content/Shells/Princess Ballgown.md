The Princess Ballgown is an elaborate Victorian Ballgown costume for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]. It's designed to be worn over her [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]] and with heavy use of metal threads as laces, made to be irremovable, without the proper tools. [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] uses a special tool to welt the threads closed so that they're irremovable, as well as basically invisible, making the entire outfit seem sewn onto her body.

**Base Layer (Over [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]])**

- Fine silk stockings, reaching upper-thigh
- White opera gloves
- White leather thigh-high ballet boots, without heels, specially designed for [[Ballet Feet|en-pointe feet]], also laced with metal threads
- Cotton chemise with high neck and long sleeves
- Silk drawers with metal thread closures at waist and thighs

**Initial Layer of Petticoats**

- First starched cotton petticoat
- Second ruffled cotton petticoat
- Third silk petticoat with elaborate lace
- Fourth heavily embroidered petticoat
- All petticoats secured with metal thread closures

**Support Garments**

- Steel-boned corset, made to fit perfectly with [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s tiny waist, with metal thread lacing through reinforced eyelets
- Corset cover with metal thread closures at back
- Bustle pad, secured with metal thread attachments
- Expansive wire-frame crinoline cage (2m in diameter), secured at waist with metal thread

**Upper Petticoats (Over Crinoline)**

- Fifth silk petticoat with multiple layers of ruffles
- Sixth and final petticoat with elaborate lace and beading
- Both secured with metal thread closures

**Main Dress Components**

- Huge black, white, and golden one-piece ballgown with:
    - Fitted bodice with extremely high collar (covering entire neck)
    - Long, fitted sleeves with attached gloves (no skin visible), resulting in another layer of fabric for her arms and hands
    - Full skirt with 3m train
    - Elaborate bustle arrangement
    - Metal thread closures along the entire back
    - Additional metal thread securing points at collar and wrists
    - All openings sealed to prevent any glimpse of skin

**Overskirt and Decorative Elements**

- Draped overskirt with multiple layers
- Decorative sash secured with metal thread
- Fabric roses and other embellishments permanently attached


**Hair and Head Accessories**

- Luxurious white wig:    
    - Elaborately styled in Victorian fashion
    - Reaches past hips and falls down onto the large skirts
    - Decorated with pearls and silk flowers
    - Glued firmly to [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]]
- Large Victorian hat with:
    - Wide brim decorated with silk flowers and feathers
    - Chin straps secured with metal thread and welded shut
    - Internal padding for comfort
    - Additional securing points with metal thread

**Final Securing Elements**

- All seams reinforced for permanence
- Multiple hidden metal thread attachment points between layers
- Strategic placement of metal thread closures to prevent unauthorized removal
- All metal threads designed to require specific tools for removal
- Only [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s face (via [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]]) visible, everything else completely covered
- Total diameter of dress at widest point: approximately 2m
- Total weight of ensemble: substantial, requiring careful movement

**[[Brain Implant]] Adjustments**

- Finally, with [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s explicit instruction, [[Lumina]] makes some temporary changes to [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s personality and behaviour, to perfectly embody this extremely rich, lavish, and royal Queen or Princess she's supposed to represent.

_Note: The massive crinoline creates an impressive silhouette, making [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] appear as a true Victorian Princess, with absolutely no skin visible except for her artificial face through the [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]]._
