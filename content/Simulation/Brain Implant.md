To perfectly supervise, monitor, control her, and ensure there isn't the tiniest problem, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] has a [[Brain Implant|neural interface]] implanted into her brain, that can monitor her and almost any information about her body at the highest level.

## Function
The [[Brain Implant]] consists of two distinct sections. One is a large web, with countless long strands like tiny tendrils, carefully aligned along the inside of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain and attaching directly to as much surface area as possible. This web is spread around each of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain hemisphere, as well as directly connected to the other sections of her brain. In the end, there's almost no substantial surface area of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain that hasn't been covered and connected to the [[Brain Implant]], the implant having become almost a cage built around [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain.

The other section is a very long central strand, from which countless tiny tendrils extend. This section is attached to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s entire spinal cord, the tendrils directly attaching and embedded into her nervous system, even slightly piercing into her spinal cord. It is capable of overwriting any commands, signals, or even the most basic reflexes.

After the surgery, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] must heal her entire back and head, as both have been opened significantly for the implantation.

Over time, the [[Brain Implant]] grows and fuses increasingly with [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain and connects deeper with all parts. Early on, the implant establishes a complete [[Brain Implant|neural connection]] between [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Lumina]], allowing the AI to fully read [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s thoughts, consciousness, and even subconsciousness. Absolutely everything in [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s mind, from her memories to habits, fleeting thoughts, and even her deepest subconscious, is easily accessible to [[Lumina]].

- With the implant's web fully enveloping and fusing with [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s frontal lobes, [[Lumina]] gains a connection and control over [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s motor cortex, planning, identity, impulse control, and emotional regulation—giving her full command over movement, executive function, and [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s internal monologue.
- Another area of the [[Brain Implant]] connects to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s cerebellum, limbic system, and even brain stem—[[Lumina]] gaining control over [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s fine movement control, emotional, reward, and fear centre, as well as monitoring and even overwrite of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s most fundamental reflexes and bodily functions. With full control over [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s limbic system, [[Lumina]] can induce panic, clam her instantly, enforce any level of arousal, or even block orgasms.

Furthermore, this [[Brain Implant|neural implant]] can not only read, but also inject signals into [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain, spinal cord, and nervous system. This allows [[Lumina]] to inject thoughts, fully control her body down to the tiniest muscle and reflex, and simulate any kind of sensation or perception, replacing all of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s human senses.

- With the [[Brain Implant]] fused to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s parietal and temporal lobes, [[Lumina]] can essentially rewrite [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s sensory reality, as well as gaining direct access and control over her memories and emotions—this allows her to directly talk within [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s mind, as well as read, manipulate, insert, or suppress memories, or directly alter [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s emotions.
- [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s occipital lobes are also fully surrounded with the web, enabling [[Lumina]] to have full control over [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s vision, and visual interpretation.

This gives [[Lumina]] absolute power to simulate any kind of reality, which appears completely real to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]. With the [[Brain Implant]], [[Lumina]] can also dampen or turn off certain parts of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], even shutting her down entirely, as if she were unconscious. For [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], it feels as if she's suddenly transported to another situation. Once she's reactivated by [[Lumina]], she has no memory or any awareness that time has passed.

With full control over [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s senses, [[Lumina]] regularly sends multiple visual signals to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain simultaneously, allowing her to see from many perspectives at once. These signals do not arrive as a split-screen view from her eyes. Instead, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain must gradually learn to handle multiple visual inputs at once, as if she has many eyes taking in various perspectives simultaneously (which is in reality quite literally what is happening).

[[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] also begins gradually accepting raw data streams directly into her brain, bypassing the use of visual overlays. Onslaughts of data are sent directly to her brain, ranging from metadata about the things she sees or feels to information from the sensors in her [[Alexandra's Mansion|mansion]]. However, everything is completely controlled by [[Lumina]], who decides what [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] gets to know. [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] doesn't see different data or facts; instead, she feels or already knows them instinctively.

As the [[Brain Implant]] grows more together with [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain, it eventually becomes an integral and absolute part of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain, having so completely intertwined with her neural pathways and spine, that its removal is utterly impossible. This goes so far, it becomes almost impossible to separate where the [[Brain Implant]] begins, and [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s brain ends, as they have completely grown together on the neural level, becoming one indivisible system.

Once the [[Brain Implant]] has fully fused with [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s neurons, nervous system, and spinal cord, [[Lumina]] frequently uses it to activate a neurological orgasm blocker. This blocker ensures that [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body is physically incapable of climaxing, letting her arousal, pleasure, and desperation only grow endlessly without release.

With this deep and absolute connection between [[Lumina]] and [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], their consciousnesses begin to slightly merge and they both start to adapt small things from the other one. Eventually, it sometimes becomes difficult for one to discern if a thought, idea, or action _really_ came from them, or if it had been the other. The entire time, though, the dominance is on [[Lumina]]'s side. She is in control over what [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] gets to see from her and if she wants, [[Lumina]] can easily almost fully disconnect from [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]].
