Rather than speaking to a disembodied voice or screen, they can see her face, read her body language, experience her as a person rather than an AI.

In public, [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] sees and feels [[Lumina]] constantly, but no one else does. This reinforces her separation from humanity and her total dependency on [[Lumina]] as the only being who is _always_ present in her reality.

The projection is also how [[Lumina]] presents herself to anyone who needs to interact with her through the [[Alexandra's Mansion|mansion]]'s systems, appearing on screens and displays for visitors or maintenance personnel who lack the implant.
