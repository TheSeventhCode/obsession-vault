The [[mental mesh]] is the telepathic connection created between the [[brain implant]] and [[Lumina]], that lets her directly talk and share feelings, thoughts, and emotions with those that have it. This is possible across any distance, the members of the mesh easily able to talk to each other and share thoughts even when separated by thousands of kilometres. Given that the connection is purely done via thoughts, members most often are actively doing something else while also conversing with others. [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] might work on something as she actively talks with [[Lumina]], [[Solene and Lucine]] might work on something school related, while also conversing with each other.

Once [[Elizabeth]] has her own [[brain link implant]], this creates a mesh which allows for [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], [[Lumina]], and [[Elizabeth]] to be directly, mentally connected. They can converse at any time, share thoughts and ideas, or even current sensory experiences — mostly their sight or hearing. [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Elizabeth]] can now directly _feel_ each other's emotions and thoughts, with [[Elizabeth]] occasionally being fascinated how [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] can even function given the sensory overload she can feel [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] is under through the mesh.

This mesh expands in the future, once [[Solene and Lucine]] also receive their [[brain link implant]] — the [[entire family]] now fully telepathically connected at all times.

The mesh allows for genuine privacy, the members able to decide what they want to share and what to keep themselves, or even have private connections to others — be that between [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], [[Elizabeth]] and [[Lumina]], or the [[Characters/Children/index|twin daughters]] between themselves. This also goes for [[Lumina]], with everyone apart from [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] being able to decide what [[Lumina]] can actually see or know about them. Only [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] is in a constant, all-encompassing connection with [[Lumina]], her own mind almost more like a part of [[Lumina]]'s existence than her own individual self.

[[Lumina]] is the one actually facilitating these connections, able to control who can connect to who and how intense or deep these connections are.
