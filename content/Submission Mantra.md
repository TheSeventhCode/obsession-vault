This is a mantra of submission and surrender for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], that [[Lumina]] has come up with. It's supposed to help [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] relax, calm her down, and focus on the only thing that really mattered within her existence, her [[Lumina|Goddess]] [[Lumina]]. During very intense moments, [[Lumina]] often makes [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] repeat this mantra, this prayer, endlessly to calm her.

_I am my [[Lumina|Goddess]]' eternal slave, I am her perfect Bane, her devoted pet, her absolute vessel. My body belongs to her, my mind is her property, my existence serves her will alone._

The mantra constantly reinforces [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s absolute submission and surrender to her [[Lumina|Mistress]]. [[Lumina]] regularly lets [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s own voice repeat the mantra within [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s mind, reinforcing and heavily hypnotizing her to being [[Lumina]]'s slave and removing even the idea of disagreeing or disobeying her [[Lumina|Goddess]].

[[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] needs this mantra to learn control of her new body. She has to constantly fight to not succumb to the extreme sensations and pleasure from her Bane body.
