After [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s transformation into a Bane, especially her removed vocal cords from the [[alternative air supply]] and the permanent [[gag]], [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] is incapable of speaking or making any sort of vocal sounds. Still, in certain situations, she needs to be able to speak. Be that through speakers inside the [[Alexandra's Mansion|mansion]], or when simulated by various disguises like the [[Alexandra's Disguise|Skinsuit]].

For this to be possible, [[Lumina]] imitates [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s old human voice, translating [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s thoughts in real time to audible sounds. However, [[Lumina]] has significantly refined and pushed this simulated voice to its absolute sensual limit. While still recognisably [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s, it carries an absurdly rich, sultry depth and impossible acoustic fullness—sounding as though she is constantly projecting from the very bottom of a pair of powerful lungs and a resonant diaphragm. Additionally, since there is no real _breath_ involved, she never pauses for air, gets hoarse, runs out of breath mid-sentence, or sounds winded even after brisk movement. The result is an intoxicating, velvety voice that commands immediate attention, weaving a spell of deep fascination and visceral arousal in anyone who hears her speak.

And although this new voice sounds weird and embarrassing to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] herself, she eventually can barely separate the sound from herself. [[Lumina]] also regularly syncs subtle micro-vibrations from the sound output directly into [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s throat [[gag]], so when [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] “speaks”, she even physically feels as if there was a sound produced inside her, even though the sensation is an obscenely twisted joke of what it would feel like for a human.

[[Lumina]] basically never replicates [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s exact old human voice, burying the memory of it with this new version. Whenever [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] needs to somehow speak aloud for others to hear her, this is the voice they hear.
