This is a custom-built android by [[Lumina]] that acts as a proxy stand-in for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], or as a courier drone. [[Lumina]] and [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] have decided to call the model, [[Alexandra's Proxy|AlexDroid]].

This android is used by [[Lumina]] for most public contacts. Although [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Lumina]] have done whatever they can, so that the outside world doesn't require anything from [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], there still are some rare instances where the old human [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] is necessary. [[Lumina]] uses this android to handle deliveries, banking, and legal appearances, which leads people in [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s city to just believe she is very reclusive.

## Physical Appearance
- The android covered in a specialized silicone layer, giving it the perfect human appearance of the old [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]. Although still very curvy, it doesn't have the [[Figure Enhancements|extreme proportions]] of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], but instead looks like the human [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] from when she had only done her first few [[Figure Enhancements|plastic surgeries]] and started her [[Rib Removal|waist training]]. Still a ridiculous hourglass figure, clearly visible that it's not entirely natural, but far from the extremes of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s current body.
    - This artificial skin also has an integrated bio-gel micro-circulation that warms the skin and shifts colour subtly, creating natural human variations like flushing cheeks or even goose bumps, except entire digitally regulated.
    - Furthermore, it has artificial sweat glands which can a produce light epidermal sheen or an imitation of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s old scent.
    - It has integrated small vibration emitters that simulate a heartbeat and breathing, the rhythm automatically altering with speech and movement.
- Depending on which mode the android runs, it has a different eye colour:
    - When running autonomously, just imitating [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] in public, it has the usual eye colour of the old human [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], _deep blue_.
    - When [[Lumina]] is fully taking it over, they get pure white with golden irises, just like [[Lumina]].
    - In the rare case [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] is piloting the android, the irises get pitch black.
## Function
The android is part of [[Lumina]]'s systems, letting her control it completely.

- [[Lumina]] has perfectly recreated [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s past appearance so that this android can act as a human-appearing proxy for [[Lumina]].
    - Rarely, [[Lumina]] also lets [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] pilot the android, but as [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] has completely abandoned her humanity, her past appearance is now completely foreign to her. The body feels disturbingly heavy and imprecise, like a primitive puppet.
    - This replication goes so far, the android also imitates [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s old human voice and has even a replication of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s fingerprint, ensuring that even people who had known the old [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] intimately aren't able to recognize that it isn't her, or even a human.
    - Having been so deeply connected and integrated into [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s thought processes due to the [[Brain Implant]], [[Lumina]] easily replicates any behaviour of the old human [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] for this [[Alexandra's Proxy|proxy android]]. From tiny mannerisms to little habits, the android replicates all that as if it were [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] herself.
- The machine is powered by a similar miniature fusion reactor like the [[Power Supply|fusion core]] in [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s chest. This gives it basically infinite runtime, never needing to recharge (though there are mechanisms to replace the reactor fuel).
    - This allows the [[Alexandra's Proxy|proxy android]] to also link up with the various vehicles that usually require [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] to be connected to them to work. Instead of [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] herself sitting in the driver seat or being connected, the android has a connection port between the legs, just like [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]], through which the [[Alexandra's Car|Lumen]] or [[Alexandra's Motorcyle|Luminous]] can be powered.
- [[Lumina]] has created multiple instances of this android, most of them stored in the [[Lab|transformation room]] of the [[Alexandra's Mansion|mansion]].
    - One is also permanently assigned to the [[transportation truck]], being stored in between the fake cabin and the rear compartment, always ensuring there's a human-seeming [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] available.
