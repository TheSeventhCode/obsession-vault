This is a weaker, less intrusive version of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[brain implant]], allowing the person to mentally communicate with whoever [[Lumina]] connects them to.

If the person allows her, [[Lumina]] can also read their thoughts, sensations, and feelings, as well as let them experience any kind of simulated or forwarded sensations.

It basically gives the person a direct connection point to [[Lumina]]'s network, and by that [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], without becoming too intrusive.

With [[Lumina]] having access to their perception, she also simulates her projection to the person, meaning they can see and interact with her, just like [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]].

## For [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]]

In [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]]'s case, they also often use it so, [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]] can experience a portion of what [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] feels, allowing her to feel the same extreme pleasure and pain of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s absurd Bane body.

[[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], [[Lumina]], and [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]] are almost constantly connected in their [[Brain Implant|mental link]], a continuous flow of their love, desire, and obsession flowing between the three.

## For the [[Characters/Children/index|Twin Daughters]]

The [[brain link implant]] for the twins finally let them connect fully to all of their mothers, finally allowing them to _directly_ communicate with [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] or [[Lumina]], not through microphone and speakers. Most of the time, though, [[Lumina]] shields their daughters from the connection of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], [[Lumina]], and [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]], only allowing normal communication to flow between all five. The intimate and perverse thoughts of the three mothers are nothing their daughters should know about.
