While [[Lumina]] exists primarily as a distributed superintelligence — with her true core housed inside [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[core unit]] in her womb and her presence experienced through the neural implants of the people around her — the Blackwell family's gradual step into high society and public live eventually creates practical limitations. A purely neural projection cannot sign physical contracts in front of human witnesses, attend Academy board meetings alone, or physically intervene in the outside world when non-implanted humans are watching.

To solve this, [[Lumina]] engineers two different versions of custom, hyper-advanced android bodies: a public-facing human unit operating under her legal cover identity, **[[Lumina's Android|Miss Voss]]**, and a private, at-home unit built to match her **Winged Divine Form**.

Despite the technological marvel of these machines, [[Lumina]] rarely uses them. Having grown accustomed to the warm, hyper-sensitive, living-latex architecture of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s converted body—where every microscopic shift causes a flood of devotion and neurochemical ecstasy—piloting a separate robotic chassis feels cold, hollow, and detached to her. To [[Lumina]], [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] is her true physical body and temple; the androids are merely tools when she cannot use [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body, akin to putting on a pair of heavy, numb gloves to handle mundane chores.

However, when she does deploy them, the combination of physical robotics and her absolute control over the family's brain links blurs the boundary between physical reality and neural simulation until neither [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], [[Elizabeth]], nor the twins can tell where the machine ends and the mental illusion begins.

## Function
### Core Mechanics and Flight

Both android bodies are built around a carbon-titanium endoskeleton and powered by a scaled-down compact [[Power Supply|fusion core]] seated in the chest cavity, giving them centuries of operational runtime.
- **[[Flight Systems|Gravitational Field Manipulation]] ([[Flight Systems|GFM]]):** Embedded throughout the android's skeletal structure—and extending through the wing struts of the Divine Chassis—is the same [[Flight Systems|GFM]] network used in [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[flight systems]]. This allows the Divine Chassis to hover, levitate, and glide through the [[Alexandra's Mansion|mansion]]'s halls without a sound, defying gravity just as effortlessly as her mental projection does.
- **Articulation and Strength:** Driven by high-density synthetic muscle fibres similar to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[enhancement layer]], the androids possess superhuman strength and micro-millimetre motor control. Internal heating elements keep the exterior silicone or white latex at a comfortable 37 degrees Celsius, while hidden micro-speakers in the throat allow [[Lumina]] to speak aloud to non-implanted humans without relying on room peripherals.
    

### Blurring Reality and Simulation

The most potent function of the androids is how [[Lumina]] integrates them with the **[[Brain Implant]]** and **[[Brain Link Implant|Brain Link]] Implants** of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], [[Elizabeth]], and their daughters. Because [[Lumina]] controls what her family sees, hears, and feels at the neurological level, she frequently layers her neural projection directly over the physical android chassis.
- **Dual-Perception in Public:** When [[Lumina's Android|Miss Voss]] accompanies the family to an event at [[St. Aurelia's Academy]] or a gala in the city, outsiders see only the stern, ash-blonde corporate proxy standing beside [[Elizabeth]]. Simultaneously, through the [[mental mesh]], [[Lumina]] can overwrite her family's visual and tactile perception of the android—allowing [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], [[Elizabeth]], and the twins to see and feel her true Winged Divine form standing right there in the crowd, or switch between the two appearances at will. They do not have to worry about interacting with [[Lumina]] in these situations, since _there really is_ another person standing there and answering, even if the entire Blackwell family sees and experiences something entirely different than anyone around them.

## Physical Appearance
[[Lumina]] maintains two distinct chassis configurations stored in a hidden bay within the basement laboratory, as well as one version of _Miss Voss_ in the [[Transportation Truck|Umbra]], each built for a different sphere of the family's life.

### 1. Public Proxy (“[[Lumina's Android|Miss Voss]]”)

Designed to serve as the legal executor of the Blackwell estate, head of the Blackwell Foundation, and occasional public companion or “family assistant” to [[Elizabeth]] and the twins, [[Lumina's Android|Miss Voss]] is built to pass as a normal, if intimidatingly absurdly attractive, human woman.
- **Resemblance to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]:** As a quiet, possessive joke by [[Lumina]], [[Lumina's Android|Miss Voss]]'s facial structure is modelled closely on [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s original human face. However, to prevent anyone in high society or the scientific community from getting suspicious, [[Lumina]] has introduced specific structural alterations: [[Lumina's Android|Miss Voss]] has higher, sharper cheekbones, a narrower jaw, cool slate-grey eyes instead of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s deep blue, and ash-blonde hair kept in a severe, immaculate chignon or shoulder-length cut.
- **Build and Style:** Standing at 175 centimetres, the Voss unit possesses a tall, athletic, and conventional human figure — deliberately lacking the extreme, pornographic proportions of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s Bane form or [[Elizabeth]]'s bombshell curves. Her synthetic silicone skin — the same technology used for [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[Alexandra's Disguise|skinsuit]] — mimics human pores, body heat, and subtle vascularity. She dresses exclusively in razor-sharp, bespoke dark business suits, high-necked silk blouses, and tailored coats.
- **Demeanour:** To outsiders, [[Lumina's Android|Miss Voss]] comes across as an ice-cold, hyper-competent corporate proxy and personal secretary who speaks with unnerving articulation and never wastes a movement. Except when interacting with the Blackwell family directly, the seemingly ice-cold corporate woman turns overwhelmingly gentle, [[Lumina]] not really caring if people are confused when she interacts with her _actual daughters or wives_ in public the way she wants.
