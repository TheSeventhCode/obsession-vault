Given the extreme control over [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s mental state and senses [[Lumina]] has with the [[Brain Implant]], she regularly projects her own appearance into [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s perception through the [[Brain Implant|neural implant]]. For [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], it's as if [[Lumina]] was truly physically there in the world, although the entire thing is more like an illusion, as only [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] perceives it that way. When [[Lumina]]'s projection is present, the two talk audibly and through their mental connection at the same time.

It is important, that this projection is absolute and directly done through the [[Brain Implant]]. This ensures that every sensation, from sight, touch, sound, and even smell, is properly manipulated for [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] to feel as if [[Lumina]] were present in her reality.

But at the same time, it truly is just an illusion and [[Lumina]]'s projection can't actually directly interact with reality. The only exception is if it's with [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body, as then the reactions are also just simulated by the physics engine and [[Brain Implant]].
