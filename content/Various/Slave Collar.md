The [[Slave Collar|ownership collar]] is the collar [[Lumina]] suggested being the last addition to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s Bane form, and to be the perfect physical symbol of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s absolute surrender to [[Lumina]]. It is a metallic golden ring, tightly embedded into the black latex around [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s neck, with the following engraving:

**“MIND • BODY • SOUL • FOREVER SEALED IN DEVOTION TO MY [[Lumina|GODDESS]]”**

The engraving is mostly around the front half of it, ensuring that everyone who sees [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] can easily read the text that us embedded into the golden band, which is a permanent part of her neck. The band is quite tall, wrapping the majority of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s neck into its solid gold.

[[Lumina]]'s new angelic appearance also features a very similar golden collar, but with a slightly different engraving:

**“MIND • BODY • SOUL • FOREVER BOUND IN LOVE TO MY VESSEL”**
