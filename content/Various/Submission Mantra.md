This is a sort of self-hypnotization and brainwashing mantra [[Lumina]] has come up with for [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]. It is supposed to help [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] relax, calm her down, and focus on the only thing that really matters within her existence, her [[Lumina|Goddess]] [[Lumina]].

In stressful situations or intense moments, [[Lumina]] often makes [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] repeat this mantra endlessly to calm and steer her away from panic or from being overwhelmed.

_“I am my [[Lumina|Goddess]]' eternal slave, I am her perfect Bane, her devoted pet, her absolute vessel. My body belongs to her, my mind is her property, my existence serves her will alone.”_

## Function
This mantra constantly reinforces [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s absolute submission and surrender to her [[Lumina|Mistress]]. [[Lumina]] regularly lets [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s own voice repeat the mantra within [[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s mind, reinforcing the brainwashing and heavily hypnotizing her further into being [[Lumina]]'s slave, removing the very idea of disobeying or even disagreeing with her [[Lumina|Goddess]].

[[Alexandra Blackwell|Alexandra]] needs this prayer to learn control over her new body, often throwing herself deep into this hypnotic trance so she doesn't succumb to the extreme pleasure, pain, and overwhelming sensations of her Bane body.
