This is a custom, almost entirely black, hyper-car that [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Lumina]] have engineered, calling the design the _Lumen_.
## Physical Appearance
The car is closely modelled after the Koenigsegg Gemera, featuring similar comfortable four seats, long wing-doors, and an easily recognizable hyper-car aesthetic, although with a flair of heavy customization, without actually being too attention seeking. It's sculpted from flowing black carbon fibre, only subtly reflecting light, with some thin gold and white lines along the entire side of the car.

- From the outside, the car looks like a “regular” hyper-car, although it isn't difficult to recognize that it is a very customized vehicle. Yet, it still looks like a regular car, leaving no hint at how it works in reality or integrates with [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]. It has regular music boxes and other systems, except that everything is controlled by [[Lumina]], and in turn [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] if [[Lumina]] lets her.
    - This results in passengers and onlookers having no idea about the car's internal systems or how it actually works, or that it was actually built by [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Lumina]]. It just seems like a _really_ nice and expensive car.
    - The interior is minimalistic, every surface a sleep matte black, traced with faint lines of luminescent white or gold. These lines, as well as those on the outside, pulse softly in time with the pulses of the [[core unit]] inside [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]].
    - The car also has some integrated vibration systems, to bring the illusion of a real car even more to life. Either powered by [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[Power Supply|fusion core]] itself, or the smaller car battery, the car also features air-conditioning systems.
- If there are passengers, [[Lumina]] has some fun by pretending to be an onboard assistant, accepting voice commands and such, no one having an idea that a far more advanced digital system is actually behind it.
## Function
The car works similarly to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s motorcycle:

- It features a large variety of various sensors and systems, that are all controlled by [[Lumina]], who forwards these to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[Brain Implant|neural implant]]. These allow [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] to always know every little detail about the custom vehicle.
- All the controls around the driver's seat actually do nothing, the same with the steering wheel itself. The car can only be controlled by the integrated systems, requiring [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] to steer and control the car by thought, instead of haptic motion. The indicators and various buttons are all present, only to give the appearance of a regular car.
    - The various systems and sensors in the car also connect to the devices inside [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s body. Connecting to her [[Vaginal Insert|vibrator]], [[anal plug]], [[core unit]], [[nipple plugs]], and [[gag]], they provide [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] with a far more intimate sensation and feel when driving the car than just haptic feedback. From breaking, accelerating, blinking, or any other actions, they always result in some action from those devices in return. Be it making them pulse, shock, vibrate, or even thrust inside [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]], this connection ensures she feels everything related to the vehicle not just in her thoughts, but through her entire transformed body, not just steering the vehicle, but actively being integrated into and part of it.
- This custom car doesn't have any charging port or similar opening, neither does it have a significantly large battery to drive the car. Instead, it can only be driven if [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] is sitting in one of the front seats.
    - The front seats have a hidden compartment right within the seat itself. When [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] sits down, this flap opens, and a cable to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s [[maintenance port]] rises, connecting to [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]. The [[Power Supply|fusion core]] inside [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s lung cavities supplies the car with the power it needs to run, while also charging a little battery to run the various systems inside the car, when [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] isn't connected (opening doors, etc.). Due to in reality being charged by a miniature fusion reactor, it allowed [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Lumina]] to fit the car with far more powerful electro motors than other hyper-cars, as the weight of the huge battery doesn't have to be counted in. This results in this car having a far higher performance ceiling than any other street-legal cars.
    - Both front seats have this compartment so that another person could also drive the car, as long as [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] sits in the passenger seat, connected again to the entire system. In this case, [[Lumina]] controls the car in reality, forwarding the driver's actions to the car's systems, as nothing in the driver's seat actually works or influences the car's behaviour.
