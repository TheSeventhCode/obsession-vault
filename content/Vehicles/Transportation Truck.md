This is a custom-built, medium-sized, unmarked matte black logistic truck. It appears completely mundane and could easily pas for a moving van or equipment hauler.

Once fully extended, it is large enough for another person (that being fully intended to be [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]]) to comfortably sleep on it, right next to the vacuum sealed [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]. This allows [[Elizabeth Carter|Elizabeth]] to travel with [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] for any sort of journey, not requiring any hotels or other sleeping arrangements.
