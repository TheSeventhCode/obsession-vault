Although most of [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]]'s life takes place entirely within her [[Alexandra's Mansion|mansion]] and on her property, where she's completely shut off from the public, later on [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] and [[Lumina]] decide to build special vehicles that integrate deliberately with her already heavily transformed body. This allows her to move around in public, go on motorcycle rides, or for [[Lumina]] to transport [[Alexandra Rose Blackwell|Alexandra]] wherever they want, enabling them to travel anywhere for any duration.
